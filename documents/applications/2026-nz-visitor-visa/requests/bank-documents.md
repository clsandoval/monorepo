# Bank documents needed

What the application requires from your bank account:

1. **Bank Certificate** — official letter on bank letterhead showing:
   - Your full name (must match passport exactly)
   - Account number and account type
   - **Current balance**
   - Dated as recently as possible (≤1 month old at submission)

2. **Bank statements** — last **6 months** of transaction history (INZ minimum is 3; 6 shows a steadier balance). Same account as the certificate.

**Format:** official PDFs (digitally signed or letterhead scans). Merge multi-page statements into one PDF.

**Context:** funds bar for this trip is low (~NZD 400/month, accommodation prepaid) — a steady, healthy balance is plenty.
