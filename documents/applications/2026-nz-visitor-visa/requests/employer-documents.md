# Employment documents needed (via Marigold)

Source: [[marigold-manufacturing]] (Mama Sita's). What the application requires:

1. **Certificate of Employment (COE)** — on company letterhead showing:
   - Your position / title
   - Employment start date (tenure)
   - Salary
   - That employment is current / ongoing
   - Dated recently (≤1 month old at submission)

2. **Approved leave letter** — confirming approved time off for **30 July – 14 August 2026** and that you return to your role afterward. (This is the strongest "intent to return" signal.)

3. **Payslips / ITR** *(optional)* — recent payslips or latest ITR as income backup, if available.

**Format:** PDFs on letterhead. Addressed generically ("To whom it may concern" / "Immigration New Zealand") is fine.
