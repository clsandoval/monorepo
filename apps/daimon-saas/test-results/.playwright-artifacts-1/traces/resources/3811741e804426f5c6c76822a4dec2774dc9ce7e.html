Setup

Live in three steps.

No infrastructure to manage. No workflows to configure. Just connect your keys and go.

01

Create your Discord bot

Head to the Discord Developer Portal and create a new application. Enable the Message Content Intent. Copy your bot token and your server (guild) ID.

02

Paste your keys

Sign up for Daimon, then paste your Discord bot token, guild ID, and Anthropic API key into the dashboard. That's it — Daimon stores them encrypted. Your keys never leave our database unencrypted.

03

Your bot goes live

Within seconds, your bot connects to your server. Mention it in any channel and it picks the right tools automatically — no commands, no configuration. Claude handles the rest.
