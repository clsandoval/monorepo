and every existing call to it stays.**

`output.succession_type` against `expected_type`, and `output.per_heir_shares.len()` against
`expected_rows`. Each message names the label and both values. The row-count assertion is what makes
a duplicated or dropped heir row a test failure rather than a silent change.
