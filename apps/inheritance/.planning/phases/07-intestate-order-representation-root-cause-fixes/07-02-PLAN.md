<objective>
Make collateral succession through a predeceased sibling correct, closing LAW-02.

Measured on 2026-07-31, the committed input
`engine/examples/defect-cases/01-collateral-halfblood-nephews.json` — one living full-blood sibling,
one predeceased half-blood sibling with two living nephews, estate 600000000 centavos — makes the
checked entry point reject its own output:

    engine output check failed: sum conservation violated: per-heir net_from_estate totals
      480000000 centavos, distributable estate is 600000000 centavos
    engine output check failed: duplicate heir_id in per_heir_shares: n1 appears 2 times
    engine output check failed: duplicate heir_id in per_heir_shares: n2 appears 2 times

Two mechanisms produce that. First, `engine/src/step2_lines.rs` anchors only degree 1, and a sibling
sits at degree 2, so no collateral ever acquires `represented_by` and no nephew is ever marked
`InheritanceMode::Representation`. Second, `distribute_collaterals` at
`engine/src/step7_distribute.rs:818-821` therefore selects its `siblings` set as
`inherits_by == OwnRight && blood_type.is_some()`, which admits a **predeceased** sibling and also
admits a **nephew** who carries a `blood_type`. Because `sib2.represented_by` is left empty, step 9
then treats the dead sibling as an unresolved vacancy and its Priority-2 branch pushes a fresh row
per representative without checking for an existing one, paying `n1` and `n2` a second time.

Fixing step 2 removes the step 9 double payment without editing step 9 at all:
`collect_vacancies` at `engine/src/step9_vacancy.rs:510` already skips a predeceased heir whose
`represented_by` is non-empty.

The target for the defect case is the figure recorded in `.planning/research/LEGAL-CONFORMANCE.md`
§2a. Art. 1005 gives surviving siblings per capita and nephews per stirpes; Art. 1006 gives the full
blood double the half blood; Art. 1008 carries that ratio into the representatives. One full-blood
line worth 2 units plus one half-blood line worth 1 unit is 3 units of 200000000 centavos each, so
`sib1` takes 400000000 and `n1` and `n2` take 100000000 each. Three rows, no duplicates, sum exactly
600000000.

This plan also handles the one lawyer-blocked boundary the fix approaches, exactly as
`07-RESEARCH.md` section 5 specifies, and it deletes the now-stale LAW-02 entry from
`engine/defect-baseline.json`, because `engine/tests/defect_ledger.rs` reports
`STALE DEFECT DECLARATION` the moment the fix lands and the plan that causes that signal is the plan
that closes it.
</objective>

return `Some`.

**The `CollateralGroup` arm** of `anchor_ids_for_category`: collect the distinct
`degree_from_decedent` values present among heirs of that category, sort them ascending, and return
the ids of every heir of that category at the **first** degree for which `degree_yields_a_line` is
true. Return an empty vector when no degree yields a line. In the ordinary family this selects the
siblings at degree 2; when every sibling record is absent from the tree it selects the nephews at
degree 3, which is Art. 975 ¶2's "if they alone survive" shape.

**Limit collateral representation to one level.** `find_representatives_recursive` recurses through
`children` with no depth limit. Art. 972 ¶2 confines collateral representation to "the children of
brothers or sisters", so a grand-nephew may not step into a predeceased nephew's place. Add a
sibling function used only for collateral anchors:

    fn find_collateral_representatives(heir: &Heir, all_heirs: &[Heir]) -> Vec<HeirId>

It walks `heir.children` exactly one level: a child that has repudiated is excluded (Art. 977), a
child that is alive and eligible and carries no representation trigger is a representative, and a
child that carries a trigger is simply skipped rather than recursed into. Have `build_single_line`
call this function instead of `find_representatives_recursive` when
`anchor.effective_category == EffectiveCategory::CollateralGroup`, and leave the descendant path on
`find_representatives_recursive` unchanged.
