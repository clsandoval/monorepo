<objective>
Put an attorney attribution block on the exported report: counsel's name, Roll of Attorneys number,
IBP roll number, PTR number and MCLE compliance number, each under its own fixed label, and loud
when any of them is missing.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 23 success criterion 2 and requirement INST-02. `23-RESEARCH.md` §1 measured
that no attribution block exists anywhere in `EstatePDF`; the nearest thing is a single 8-point grey
line inside the letterhead reading
`Alpha Attorney | IBP Roll No. IBP-000002 | PTR No. PTR-000003 | MCLE No. MCLE-000004`, which was
confirmed by rendering the real document. That line is unlabelled per field, is dropped whole when
`counselName` is empty, and cannot state that a credential is missing. An instrument that a lawyer
signs states who signed it, under what numbers, and says so when it cannot.

Output: a new PDF section rendered unconditionally, the letterhead reduced to firm identity so
nothing is stated twice, and tests for every path.
</objective>

<constraints>
- The block is ALWAYS rendered. It is not gated on `profile`, not gated on any `PDFExportOptions`
  field and not gated on a value being present. A report whose attribution is absent must say so on
  its own face; silently omitting the block would make an unsigned report indistinguishable from a
  signed one.
- The five labels are fixed module constants and are exactly:
  `Counsel:`, `Roll of Attorneys No.:`, `IBP Roll No.:`, `PTR No.:`, `MCLE Compliance No.:`.
  `IBP Roll No.` is copied verbatim from the label the settings form already uses, so a stored value
  keeps the name it was entered under.
- A field that is `null` or the empty string renders its label followed by the literal
  `NOT ON FILE`. No field is omitted and no field is silently blank.
- When `profile` is `null` the block renders its heading and one line, the literal
  `ATTORNEY ATTRIBUTION UNAVAILABLE — no firm profile was loaded for this export.`, and no
  label lines.
- The counsel credentials line is REMOVED from `FirmHeaderSection`, so the letterhead states firm
  identity and the attribution block states counsel identity, and neither repeats the other. The
  existing assertions in `pdf.test.tsx` that cover that line are RELOCATED to
  `attribution.test.tsx` against the component that now owns the behaviour — never deleted, and the
  count of assertions covering counsel credentials does not fall.
- No logo is rendered. `23-RESEARCH.md` §3 measured that `EstatePDF` never passes `logoDataUrl`, that
  no seeded row carries a `logo_url`, and that no `INST-*` requirement names a logo. The
  `logoDataUrl` prop and its branch stay exactly as they are.
- This plan writes no article literal and no legal conclusion. It prints stored identifiers.
- `bash scripts/ci-gates.sh` exits 1 on this branch for reasons this phase does not own. This plan
  runs the scoped commands below and makes no claim about the whole suite.
</constraints>
