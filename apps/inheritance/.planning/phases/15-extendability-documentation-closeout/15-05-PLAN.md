<objective>
Plans `15-01` through `15-04` each built a check and deliberately did not register it. This plan
registers all four, writes their `GATES.md` sections, points `README.md` at the two new procedures,
closes EXT-05 through EXT-08 in `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`, and proves the whole suite green at 32
gates.

The ordering constraint is inherited and measured, not invented. `scripts/check-gate-results.mjs`
(**G9**) fails with `RESULTS INCOMPLETE` when it sees any gate other than itself as `not-run`, and the
runner republishes after every gate — so a gate ordered after G9 would fail G9 on every run. G9 has
therefore stayed last since Phase 4, with `G8` immediately before it. Phases 13 and 14 each inserted
their new gates ahead of `G10` and shifted `G10`, `G11`, `G8`, `G9` down. This plan repeats that
placement exactly.

The four new checks are static, dependency-free and complete in about two seconds combined, so their
placement costs nothing in runtime. They take orders 25 through 28, immediately after Phase 14's
`G29` and immediately before `G10`.

`G14` remains reserved and unregistered. Phase 9's plan `09-06` is still blocked, and taking its id
would make that plan unlandable.
</objective>
