<objective>
Delete both duplicate scenario classifiers and back the "Predicted:" badge with the engine.

`09-RESEARCH.md` §1.1 found three classifiers. Two of them are in the frontend: a dead one in
`bridge.ts` reachable only from the equally dead `computeMock`, and a live one in `ReviewStep.tsx`
that renders the badge. §1.2 measured the live one against the engine on the two fixtures its own
tests use: it says `I1` where the engine says `I2`, and `T1` where the engine says `T2`. Both are off
by one code on the most common Philippine family shape.

badge to that entry point, and strengthens the three badge tests from prefix regexes to the exact
codes the engine returns.

being wrong, and three tests that would have caught this defect.
