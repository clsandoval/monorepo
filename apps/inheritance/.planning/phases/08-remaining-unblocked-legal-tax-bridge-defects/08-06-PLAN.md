<objective>
Hand the succession engine the estate the heirs actually divide, instead of net taxable estate minus
tax.

`frontend/src/lib/estate-tax-engine/pipeline.ts:575-576` publishes
`item40_gross_estate: netTaxableEstate` and `item44_total_deductions: taxComputation.netEstateTaxDue`.
Both names are false, and `frontend/src/lib/tax-bridge.ts:46-51` then computes
`max(0, item40 − item44)`. Net taxable estate is already net of the ₱5,000,000 standard deduction, the
family-home deduction, the RA 4917 deduction and the vanishing deduction — none of which is property
that left the estate.

Art. 908 sets the base as the property left at death "deducting all debts and charges." A deduction
"allowed without need of substantiation" is neither a debt nor a charge; neither is a family home that
passes to the heirs; neither is relief for property previously taxed.

`.planning/research/LEGAL-CONFORMANCE.md` §6 verified the tax side of the worked example end to end: a
₱30,000,000 all-conjugal estate with a ₱12,000,000 conjugal family home, a surviving spouse and two
children, death 2024-03-01, produces gross ₱30,000,000, Item 39 spouse share ₱15,000,000, Item 40 NTE
₱4,000,000 and a tax of ₱240,000. §2a then measured the bridge: it hands the succession engine
**₱3,760,000**, giving each of the three heirs ₱1,253,333, where the correct base is
30,000,000 − 15,000,000 − 240,000 = **₱14,760,000** and each heir takes **₱4,920,000**. A 74.5%
understatement of every heir's share.

Output: four named Art. 908 components published by the tax pipeline, a bridge that assembles them and
throws when one is missing, and the worked example pinned to the centavo.
</objective>
