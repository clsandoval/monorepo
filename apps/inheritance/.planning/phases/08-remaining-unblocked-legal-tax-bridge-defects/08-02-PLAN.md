<objective>
Stop granting the ₱500,000 medical-expense deduction that RA 10963 (TRAIN) repealed, and correct the
three places that currently certify the repealed answer.

RA 10963 Sec. 23 deleted funeral, judicial **and** medical expenses from NIRC Sec. 86(A) together, and
RR 12-2018 Sec. 6 is an exhaustive nine-item list in which a case-insensitive search for "medical"
returns nothing. `frontend/src/lib/estate-tax-engine/special-deductions.ts:106-115` gates neither: its
`computeMedicalDeduction` does not even accept `deductionRules`, while its two siblings
`computeFuneralDeduction` at `:82` and `computeJudicialAdminDeduction` at `:99` both carry
`if (deductionRules === 'TRAIN') return 0;`. Measured effect on a 2024 death:
₱500,000 of deduction, ₱30,000 of understated tax per estate.

Two other sites certify the same wrong rule. `advisor.ts:231-262` recommends claiming the deduction
and does so **only** when `dateOfDeath >= '2018-01-01'` — the exact regime where it no longer exists;
the date gate is inverted relative to the statute. And `specs/estate-tax-engine-spec.md:1774` gives
golden test TV-02 a 2023 date of death and then allows ₱400,000 of medical, so the spec locks in a
tax of ₱111,000 where the statute produces ₱135,000.

Output: one regime gate, one inverted advisor gate corrected, six test expectations corrected to the
statute, and a TV-02 vector whose five affected rows agree with the law.
</objective>
