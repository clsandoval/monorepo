<objective>
Include Transfers for Public Use in the vanishing-deduction reduction ratio, in both the TRAIN and the
pre-TRAIN branch.

NIRC Sec. 86(A)(5) as amended computes the reduction against "the amounts allowed as deductions under
paragraphs (2), (3), (4), **and (6)**", and paragraph (6) is Transfers for Public Use. RA 8424
Sec. 86(A)(2) pointed at "paragraphs (1) and (3)", and pre-TRAIN paragraph (3) was also Transfers for
Public Use. `frontend/src/lib/estate-tax-engine/ordinary-deductions.ts:361-364` includes neither, so
both branches are short by the same term:

```
  const elitForVD =
    deductionRules === 'PRE_TRAIN'
      ? elitBase + funeral.total + judicial.total
      : elitBase;
```

`.planning/research/LEGAL-CONFORMANCE.md` §2b records the worked example: a ₱30,000,000 estate with
₱1,000,000 of claims, a ₱5,000,000 transfer for public use and ₱10,000,000 of qualifying property
produces a vanishing deduction of ₱9,666,666 today where the statute requires ₱8,000,000, and a tax of
₱560,000 where the statute requires ₱660,000. That row carries the explicit fixer note "Add
`publicTransfers.total` unconditionally."

The change is an **ordering** change, not a signature change. `computePublicUseTransfers` is currently
called at `:375`, after `computeVanishingDeduction` at `:367`; it depends only on `input` and
`nraFactor`, so moving it above introduces no cycle. Nine tests in
`__tests__/ordinary-deductions.test.ts` call `computeVanishingDeduction` directly with a literal third
argument, and changing its arity would churn all nine for no gain.

Output: the reordered computation, one new worked-example test per regime, and a spec section that
names paragraph (6).
</objective>
