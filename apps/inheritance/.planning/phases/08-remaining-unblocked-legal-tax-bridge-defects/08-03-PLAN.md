<objective>
Make preterition do what Art. 854 says it does: annul the institution of heirs, **but** keep the
devises and legacies valid insofar as they are not inofficious.

`engine/src/step7_distribute.rs:176` handles `SuccessionType::Intestate` and
`SuccessionType::IntestateByPreterition` in one match arm that calls `compute_intestate_distribution`
over the whole `estate_base` and never reads `will.legacies`. `08-RESEARCH.md` §1.2 reproduced the
consequence on a purpose-built input: E=₱30,000,000, two legitimate children, a ₱3,000,000 legacy to
`carlos` against a ₱15,000,000 free portion — the engine returns `ana=1500000000`, `ben=1500000000`
and **no row for `carlos` at all**. He is paid zero, silently.

Seven committed inputs reproduce this shape today: `examples/testate-cases/08.json` and
`examples/fuzz-cases/061`, `062`, `066`, `067`, `068`, `069`. Their per-heir amounts are pinned by no
test and by no gate; `engine/examples/test-results.md` and `testate-test-results.md` are written by
`generate-test-cases.sh` and read by nothing.

The machinery this needs already exists and is already tested. `add_fp_to_distributions`
(`step7_distribute.rs:393`) creates a legatee row; the testate arm at `:327-351` already reads reduced
legacy amounts out of `validation.inofficiousness.reductions`; and `check_inofficiousness`
(`step6_validation.rs:496`) already computes the Art. 911 reduction. The only reason none of it is
reachable on the preterition path is that the early return at `step6_validation.rs:201-220` hardcodes
a zeroed `InofficiousnessResult` instead of calling the check.

Output: a real inofficiousness result on the preterition path, a dedicated preterition distribution arm
that pays surviving legacies before dividing the residue, and a flag for the dispositions the engine
cannot value.
</objective>
