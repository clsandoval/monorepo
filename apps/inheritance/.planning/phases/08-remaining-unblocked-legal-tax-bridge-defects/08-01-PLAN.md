<objective>
Stop preterition from firing on a compulsory heir who received an advance on their legitime, and make
the one case that is genuinely arguable loud instead of silent.

`.planning/research/LEGAL-CONFORMANCE.md` records the defect: `heir_addressed_in_will`
(`engine/src/step6_validation.rs:829`) reads only the will's four clause lists and never consults
`input.donations`, so a child holding a donation *inter vivos* is treated as preterited and the whole
will is destroyed. `08-RESEARCH.md` §1.2 reproduced it: 1 legitimate child `ben`, a ₱10,000,000
donation to `ben` with every exemption flag `false`, a will instituting a stranger to the entire free
portion — the engine returns `IntestateByPreterition` and gives `ben` the whole ₱30,000,000 estate.

*Morales v. Olondriz* states the test as "the omission is total, meaning the heir did not also receive
any legacies, devises, **or advances on his legitime**." Art. 1061 defines an advance as property
received from the decedent by donation and brought into the mass "in order that it may be computed in
the determination of the legitime." Five articles then declare specific donations not brought to
collation at all. This plan implements only what those sentences force, and records the remainder as a
question.

Output: a donation-aware preterition scope, a new manual-review flag for the exempted case, and a
ninth recorded decision `LAWYER-09` wired into gate G10 in both directions.
</objective>
