<objective>
Turn the engine's warning channel and its per-step computation log back on, end to end.

Purpose: `engine/src/step10_finalize.rs:619` hardcodes `warnings: vec![]`, and `Step10Input` has no
field through which a prior step's flag could arrive. Measured over the 140 committed inputs, zero
cases produce a warning and every case returns a `computation_log` with exactly one entry. Seven of
the ten steps build `ManualFlag` values on their own output struct and `engine/src/pipeline.rs`
discards all of them. Until this is fixed, every later legal fix in Phases 7, 8 and 14 lands
invisibly: a lawyer reading the output cannot tell a corrected case from a broken one.

This is the first plan in the project to change the shape of `Step10Input`, and the structural fact
that governs the whole task is recorded in `05-RESEARCH.md` section 3: `engine/tests/integration.rs`
does not call `pipeline::run_pipeline`. It defines its own private copy at line 27 and constructs
`Step10Input` at lines 147-163. A field added on one side and not the other does not compile.

Output: a `Step10Input` that carries `warnings` and `step_logs`, a `pipeline.rs` that fills both on
the normal path and the restart path, an `integration.rs` mirror, and Rust tests proving a flag
built in step 6 is visible in `EngineOutput`.
</objective>
