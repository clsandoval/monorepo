<objective>

modules as attribution display layers.

ordinarily identifies specific property — TCT number, area, Registry of Deeds — adjudicated
to each heir. The succession engine consumes exactly one money scalar
(`EngineInput.net_distributable_estate.centavos`) and holds no asset schedule at all, so it

`DISPLAY_LAYERS`, seven entries today, and its own comment says "Adding a display layer means
adding it here." Four new files render or carry attribution and none of them is listed yet.

appending.
</objective>
