Interaction Patterns

• Accordion behavior: Completed sections collapse to a summary line. Active section is expanded. Upcoming sections show title only. Click any section to expand/edit.

• Progress bar: Thin segmented line at top — fills as sections complete. Not sticky (stays at top of page).

• Conditional fields: Fields appear/disappear within sections based on selections (e.g., compensation fields only show if "Mixed Income" is selected). No full-section hiding — all 5 sections always visible.

• Compute button: Appears at the bottom after all required fields are filled. Single button: "Compute Tax".

• No review step: The collapsed summary lines ARE the review. Scan them before hitting compute.

• Auto-save: Still debounced at 1500ms, but indicator is a subtle dot in the top bar, not a banner.
