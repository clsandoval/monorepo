How It Works

• Hero number: The single most important output — total tax due — huge and centered. No ambiguity about what this page is telling you.

• Recommendation pill: Green badge below the number. Which regime, how much you save. One line.

• Regime comparison: Always visible, compact two-row format. Recommended regime highlighted with green left dot. Shows amount + effective rate.

• Detail sections: All collapsed by default. Click to expand. Each contains the detailed breakdown (tax computation steps, quarterly amounts, penalties, etc.).

• Actions bar: Three equal buttons at bottom — PDF, Share, Edit. No hierarchy tricks, all equally accessible.

• Warnings: If there are warnings (e.g., late filing), they appear as a subtle amber banner above the hero number. Not in a detail section — warnings should be seen.
