Pattern Summary

• Every page follows the same skeleton: top bar → centered column (max-w-xl) → content. No page-specific layouts.

• List pages (computations, clients, deadlines): heading + action button + stacked rows. Each row shows name, key detail, date. Click to drill in.

• Landing page: Dead simple — logo, one-line description, sign-in form. No marketing sections, no feature lists.

• Settings: Same column, stacked form sections (personal info, team members, firm branding). No tabs — just scroll.

• Dashboard merges with computations list: No separate dashboard page. Computations list IS the home page after auth. Removes one click from the flow.

• Deadline urgency: Upcoming deadlines get a subtle red tint on the row. Past deadlines get stronger red treatment.
