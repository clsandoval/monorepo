## Design Context

### Scope

TaxKlaro — a tax calculation and regime comparison tool for Philippine small business owners and freelancers.

### Users

Small business owners and freelancers in the Philippines figuring out their tax obligations. They're not tax experts — they need to understand which tax regime saves them money and why, without needing an accountant to interpret the results. They're often anxious about getting taxes wrong and want clear, trustworthy answers fast.

### Brand Personality

**Clean, confident, sharp.** TaxKlaro should feel like a knowledgeable friend who happens to be great with numbers — not a government form, not a generic dashboard.

### Emotional Goals

Primary: **Confidence & clarity** — "I understand my taxes now." The interface should demystify complexity and feel empowering, not overwhelming.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Visual tone:** Professional but approachable. Data-dense where needed, but always clear. Strong typographic hierarchy (DM Serif Display for headings, DM Sans for body) creates editorial authority.

**References:**
- **Wise (TransferWise)** — Approachable finance, clear number presentation, friendly but serious
- **Linear** — Clean, fast, confident, no visual clutter

**Anti-references:**
- Not generic SaaS — no cookie-cutter grey/blue dashboards
- Not overly corporate — no heavy borders, badge overload, enterprise stiffness
- Not trendy/flashy — no gratuitous gradients, glassmorphism, or excessive animation

**Theme:** Light mode primary. Dark mode supported via CSS variables.

### Design Principles

1. **Numbers are the hero** — Tax amounts, savings, and comparisons should be the most prominent elements. Use size, weight, and color to make key figures unmissable.
2. **Clarity over decoration** — Every visual element must earn its place. If it doesn't help the user understand their taxes, remove it.
3. **Semantic color with restraint** — Green for savings, red for amounts due, blue for brand/neutral. Use color to convey meaning, not to decorate.
4. **Progressive disclosure** — Show the answer first, details on demand. Don't overwhelm with tax law complexity upfront.
5. **Typography creates hierarchy** — DM Serif Display for authority and anchoring, DM Sans for readable body content. Let the type system do the heavy lifting.

### Tech Stack

- Vite + React 19 + TypeScript 5
- TailwindCSS 4
- shadcn/ui (new-york style, slate base)
- TanStack Router + TanStack Query
- Fonts: DM Sans Variable (body), DM Serif Display (display)

### Color System

RGB-based with semantic meaning:
- **Brand blue:** `29 78 216` (#1D4ED8)
- **Savings (positive):** Green `21 128 61`
- **Tax due (negative):** Red `185 28 28`
- **Optimal regime:** Light green background
- **Suboptimal regime:** Light yellow background

### Accessibility

- WCAG AA minimum compliance
- Sufficient color contrast for all text and semantic colors
- Keyboard navigation support
- Screen reader compatibility
- Semantic HTML structure
