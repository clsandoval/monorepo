# Helios

20-court, 10-story sports facility. Joint venture with Robinsons Land (RLC).

## Status

- **Timeline**: ~2 years out (estimated completion ~2028)
- **Scale**: 20 courts across 10 stories
- **Partner**: Robinsons Land Corporation (RLC)
- **Technology**: Will use PodPlay — each court gets own branded app ("Helios")

## PodPlay Integration

- Helios will get its own branded PodPlay app (like Telepark, PinkPod)
- Per-facility app model: developers change color palette + logos, same PodPlay core
- Full replay system (cameras, Mac Mini, iPads, Apple TVs) per court

## Notes

- Largest planned venue in the Kosmas portfolio
- JV structure with one of the Philippines' largest property developers
- Long-horizon project — design and construction phase
