# Business Registration & Compliance — angkin.ph SEO Spec

## Hub Page
- **URL:** /business/
- **H1:** "Business Registration & Compliance Calculators & Tools — Philippines"
- **Overview:**

Philippine business registration and compliance spans four major regulatory agencies — the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) for sole proprietorship registration, the Board of Investments (BOI/PEZA) for investment incentives under the CREATE MORE Act, the Cooperative Development Authority (CDA) for cooperative registration and tax exemption, and the Philippine Contractors Accreditation Board (PCAB) for construction industry licensing. Together, these agencies touch an addressable market of 5M+ sole proprietors, 3,000-5,000 active BOI/PEZA enterprises, 25,000+ registered cooperatives, and 70,000+ contractors.

The compliance burden is multi-deadline and multi-agency: the annual January 20 business permit renewal deadline creates acute annual pressure for 1.2-1.5M business permit renewals; DTI business name registration expires every 5 years with a 50% surcharge for late renewal; BOI investment incentive elections are irrevocable and require Big-4 advisory (PHP 200,000-500,000 per engagement) under the CREATE MORE Act (RA 12066); CDA cooperative tax exemption certificates have a 60-day initial filing window that is almost universally missed; and PCAB contractor licensing uses a complex scoring formula (Financial Capacity + Equipment Capacity + Field Experience + Sustained Track Employment) that determines which project sizes a contractor can bid on.

This is the largest hub on angkin.ph with 15 tools across four agencies, serving the full lifecycle of Philippine business compliance from startup registration through ongoing annual obligations and specialized industry licensing.
