# Land, Property & Agrarian Reform — angkin.ph SEO Spec

## Hub Page
- **URL:** /land/
- **H1:** "Land, Property & Agrarian Reform Calculators & Tools — Philippines"
- **Overview:**

Philippine real property transactions involve a complex multi-agency pipeline spanning the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR), Local Government Units (LGUs), the Land Registration Authority (LRA), the Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR), and the Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD). With an estimated 400,000-800,000 real property transfer transactions per year, each requiring 2-4 separate agency filings, and professional processing services charging PHP 20,000-50,000 per transaction, this is one of the highest-stakes regulatory pipelines in the Philippines.

The ONETT (One-Time Transaction) property transfer pipeline alone carries strict statutory deadlines — a 30-day CGT filing deadline and a 5-day DST deadline from notarization — with penalties of 25% surcharge plus 12%/year interest for late filing under NIRC Sec. 248-250. The PD 1529 Sec. 117 double-fee penalty for delayed Registry of Deeds filing creates an additional trap. O-LRA-1 (ONETT Deadline Calculator) scored 4.55 after professional-fee validation — the highest-scoring tool in the entire angkin.ph platform, reflecting confirmed processing service fees of PHP 20,000-50,000 per transaction and genuine specialist dependency.

This hub also covers agrarian reform land valuation under RA 6657 (CARP) and real estate developer licensing under PD 957 (DHSUD), creating a comprehensive property lifecycle toolkit from acquisition through development and transfer.
