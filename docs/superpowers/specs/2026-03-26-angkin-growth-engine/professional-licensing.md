- **Overview:**

The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) governs the licensure, registration, and triennial renewal of 4.9 million+ licensed professionals across 43 regulated professions in the Philippines. From the 2,080,000 registered professional teachers and 966,000 nurses to 200,000+ CPAs and 252,000 criminologists, every PRC-licensed professional must navigate the Continuing Professional Development (CPD) system mandated by RA 10912 (CPD Act of 2016), track renewal deadlines for their Professional Identification Card (PIC), and manage compliance costs that range from a simple renewal fee to tens of thousands of pesos in CPD seminar expenses.

The angkin.ph PRC hub provides free, statute-backed calculators that decode the complexity of CPD compliance across all 43 Professional Regulatory Boards. Whether you are a nurse tracking your 15-unit CPD requirement, a real estate broker facing the steeper 45-unit mandate under RA 9646, or an OFW professional unaware of your full CPD exemption under RA 10912 Section 9, these tools compute your exact obligations, costs, and deadlines from the governing law itself. With 1.3-1.6 million PIC renewals processed annually and the CPD Undertaking deadline approaching on June 30, 2026, compliance demand is at an all-time high.

The PRC compliance landscape is uniquely fragmented: two separate portals (LERIS for renewal, CPDAS for CPD tracking), 43 profession-specific rule sets, unregulated seminar pricing (PHP 1,000-3,000 per CPD unit), and a 43,000-signature petition documenting professional frustration with the system. Our tools consolidate this complexity into clear, deterministic outputs grounded in RA 8981, RA 10912, and the applicable Professional Regulatory Board resolutions.

- **Hub FAQs:**

1. **How many CPD units do I need to renew my PRC license?** Most professions require 15 units per 3-year renewal cycle (reduced from 45 units in December 2022 by PRC Board Resolution). Exceptions: Real estate brokers/appraisers require 45 units (RA 9646, Section 31), social workers require 45 units (RA 10847, Section 26), and CPAs seeking BOA accreditation need 120 units under IFAC standards.

2. **Am I exempt from CPD requirements as an OFW?** Yes. Under RA 10912, Section 9(a), professionals working abroad at the time of renewal are fully exempt from CPD requirements. You can renew your PRC license without any CPD units if you provide proof of overseas employment. Many OFWs are unaware of this exemption and unnecessarily pay for overseas CPD seminars.

3. **What is the CPD Undertaking, and how long can I use it?** The CPD Undertaking allows professionals to renew their PRC license by promising to complete CPD units in the next renewal cycle. PRC has extended this option through June 30, 2026 (per PRC Memorandum Order No. 98, s. 2023). However, any deficit carries over: if you are short by N units, your next cycle requires 15 + N units.

4. **How much does PRC license renewal cost in total?** The base renewal fee is PHP 1,050 for baccalaureate professions and PHP 450 for non-baccalaureate professions (per PRC official fee schedule). Late renewal incurs a PHP 30 surcharge. On top of official fees, CPD compliance costs PHP 840-1,400 for online courses (7 units) or PHP 15,000-45,000+ for face-to-face seminars (15 units), depending on profession and provider.

5. **What is the SDL cap and why does it matter?** Self-Directed Learning (SDL) activities are capped at 30% of your total required CPD units (per PRC Resolution No. 1197, s. 2019). For most professions, that means a maximum of 4-5 SDL units out of 15. Each SDL application costs PHP 500 to file with PRC. Exceeding the cap means those excess SDL units are not credited.

6. **When should I renew my PRC license to avoid penalties?** PRC licenses are renewed based on your birth month. Late renewal incurs a 50% surcharge on the base renewal fee under PRC implementing rules. Starting renewal 3-6 months before your expiry date is recommended to allow time for CPD compliance verification.

7. **How do I know if my CPD provider is accredited?** Check the CPDAS portal (cpdas.prc.gov.ph) for accredited CPD providers. Provider accreditation is valid for 3 years. Government entities (DepEd, state universities, DOH) are accredited at no cost. As of 2024, there are 575+ accredited providers for teachers, 353+ for nurses, and 171+ for CPAs.

8. **Can I take the board exam before completing CPD?** CPD is a renewal requirement, not an examination requirement. You do not need CPD units to take the board exam. CPD compliance is triggered only when you renew your Professional Identification Card (PIC) after passing the exam. First-time licensees are exempt from CPD for their first renewal cycle.
