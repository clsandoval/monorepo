# Pag-IBIG / HDMF (Home Development Mutual Fund) — angkin.ph SEO Spec

## Hub Page

- **URL:** /pagibig/
- **H1:** "Pag-IBIG Fund Calculators & Tools — Philippines"
- **Overview:**

Pag-IBIG Fund (Home Development Mutual Fund / HDMF) is one of three mandatory social insurance funds in the Philippines, operating a mutual savings system that leverages member contributions to offer affordable housing loans and short-term cash loans. As of 2024, Pag-IBIG has 16.58 million active members, released P129.73 billion in housing loans to 90,616 homes, and disbursed P70.33 billion in cash loans to 3.2 million members. Total Fund assets exceed P1.2 trillion. The Fund is governed by Republic Act No. 9679 (Home Development Mutual Fund Law of 2009).

angkin.ph's Pag-IBIG hub provides free, statute-accurate calculators for every Pag-IBIG computation a Filipino worker, homebuyer, or employer encounters: mandatory savings contribution tables, Multi-Purpose Loan (MPL) and Calamity Loan computations, housing loan eligibility with the Affordable Housing Program, MP2 voluntary savings projections, and TAV accumulation with refund qualification. Every calculator cites the specific RA 9679 section or HDMF circular governing its formula.

The housing loan eligibility tool fills the single largest transparency gap in Philippine homeownership: most aspiring buyers do not know their maximum loanable amount, which interest rate tier they qualify for, or whether they are eligible for the 3% Affordable Housing Program — until they are already deep in a broker relationship. angkin.ph puts that information at their fingertips before they engage any intermediary.

- **Hub FAQs:**
  1. **How much is the monthly Pag-IBIG contribution in 2025?** Employees earning over P1,500/month contribute 2% of Monthly Basic Compensation, capped at P200/month (MFS ceiling P10,000 per HDMF Circular 274). Employers match with 2%, also capped at P200/month. Those earning P1,500 or below contribute 1% employee / 2% employer. (RA 9679 Sec. 9)
  2. **What is a Pag-IBIG Multi-Purpose Loan (MPL)?** The MPL is a short-term loan available to active members with at least 24 monthly contributions. Maximum loanable amount = 80% of your Total Accumulated Value (TAV). Interest rate: 10.75% per annum fixed. Term: up to 24 months (or 36 months). (RA 9679 Sec. 17)
  3. **How much can I borrow for a Pag-IBIG housing loan?** Your maximum loanable depends on income (35% HLAR cap), property value (80-95% LTV), and age (age + term cannot exceed 70 years). Interest rates range from 3% (Affordable Housing Program) to 10.5% depending on loan amount tier. Maximum loan: P6 million. (RA 9679 Sec. 14-16)
  4. **What is the Pag-IBIG MP2 savings program?** MP2 is a voluntary savings program with a 5-year lock-in period offering higher dividend rates (7.10% in 2024 vs. 6.60% regular). Tax-exempt. Minimum contribution P500 per remittance, no maximum. Choose between annual dividend payout or terminal payout (full compounding). (RA 9679 Sec. 9)
  5. **When can I withdraw my Pag-IBIG savings?** Qualifying grounds for TAV refund: maturity (240 months/20 years of contributions), retirement (age 60 optional, 65 mandatory), permanent total disability, critical illness, permanent departure from the Philippines, or death (beneficiaries claim). Outstanding loans are auto-offset from refund. (RA 9679 Sec. 19-21)
  6. **What is the penalty for late Pag-IBIG employer remittance?** 1/10 of 1% per day of unpaid contributions from the due date. This equals approximately 36.5% per year — significantly punishing for delinquent employers. (RA 9679 Sec. 22)
  7. **What is the Pag-IBIG Affordable Housing Program?** Members earning P15,000/month or less (NCR) or P12,000/month or less (non-NCR) qualify for housing loans up to P750,000 at 3% fixed interest for the first 10 years, per HDMF Circular 403. This program is chronically underutilized because qualifying borrowers do not know it exists.
  8. **What is the difference between Pag-IBIG MPL and Calamity Loan?** Both are short-term cash loans against your TAV. MPL has a 10.75% annual interest rate; Calamity Loan has 5.95% (lowest Pag-IBIG rate) with a 3-month grace period. Combined ceiling: 80% of TAV. The Calamity Loan is only available in declared calamity areas. (RA 9679 Sec. 17)
