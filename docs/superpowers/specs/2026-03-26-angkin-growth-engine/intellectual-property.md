## Hub Page
- **URL:** /ip/
- **H1:** "Intellectual Property Calculators & Tools — Philippines"
- **Overview:**

The Intellectual Property Office of the Philippines (IPOPHL) administers trademark registration, patent grants, utility model registration, and industrial design registration under Republic Act 8293 (IP Code of the Philippines). With approximately 42,000+ trademark applications, 4,500 patent applications, and 1,847 utility model applications filed annually, the Philippine IP landscape is growing rapidly — trademark filings jumped 2.5% in 2023 and are projected to exceed those levels by 7%+ in 2024. An estimated 200,000-400,000 active registered trademarks and 20,000-45,000 active patents require ongoing compliance management.

The IP compliance burden is multi-stage and multi-year: trademark holders must file Declaration of Actual Use (DAU) at Year 3 and Year 5 (failure = automatic cancellation with no cure period), renew every 10 years (50% late surcharge during grace period), and manage escalating patent annuity fees from PHP 1,550 at Year 5 to PHP 65,160 at Year 20. IP law firms charge PHP 10,000-20,000/year in retainer fees for portfolio monitoring, while enterprise docketing software (Anaqua, CPA Global) prices out SMEs entirely at USD 10,000-50,000+/year. IPOPHL's MC 2024-012 formally established trademark agent accreditation, confirming a real professional market.

These tools bring transparent fee computation, lifecycle cost forecasting, and deadline management to the 70,000+ Philippine businesses with active IP registrations — for the first time, without requiring an IP firm retainer.
