# SSS (Social Security System) — angkin.ph SEO Spec

## Hub Page

- **URL:** /sss/
- **H1:** "SSS Calculators & Tools — Philippines"
- **Overview:**

The Social Security System (SSS) is the primary social insurance institution for private-sector workers in the Philippines, covering approximately 40 million registered members — employed, self-employed, voluntary, and OFW. Under Republic Act No. 11199 (Social Security Act of 2018), SSS administers seven distinct benefit types plus mandatory employer contribution remittance, each governed by deterministic statutory formulas that are nonetheless widely misunderstood.

angkin.ph's SSS hub provides free, statute-accurate calculators for every SSS computation a Filipino worker, retiree, or employer will encounter: from monthly contribution tables and penalty exposure, to the three-formula retirement pension comparison, maternity benefit with salary differential, sickness and disability benefits, unemployment claims, and survivor's pension. Every calculator cites the specific section of RA 11199 or implementing circular that governs its formula, ensuring users can verify the law themselves.

In 2023, SSS disbursed P257.4 billion in benefits to 4.74 million beneficiaries. Despite this scale, no single third-party tool covers all SSS benefit types with a unified computation model. angkin.ph fills that gap.

- **Hub FAQs:**
  1. **What is the SSS contribution rate for 2025?** The total contribution rate is 15% of the Monthly Salary Credit (MSC), split 10% employer and 5% employee for employed members. The MSC range is P5,000 to P35,000 in P1,000 increments, with amounts above P20,000 subject to the Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF) component. (RA 11199 Sec. 18; SSS Circular 2024-06)
  2. **How is the SSS monthly retirement pension computed?** SSS uses a three-formula comparison: (1) P300 + 20% of AMSC + 2% of AMSC for each CYS over 10; (2) 40% of AMSC; (3) a floor of P1,200 (10-19 CYS) or P2,400 (20+ CYS). The highest result becomes the Basic Monthly Pension (BMP), plus a P1,000 monthly additional benefit. You need at least 120 monthly contributions for a monthly pension; otherwise you receive a lump sum. (RA 11199 Sec. 12)
  3. **How many days of maternity leave does SSS cover?** Under the Expanded Maternity Leave Law (RA 11210), SSS covers 105 days for live birth (normal or caesarean), 120 days for solo parents, and 60 days for miscarriage or emergency termination. There is no delivery frequency cap. Up to 7 days may be allocated to the father or alternate caregiver. (RA 11210; RA 11199 Sec. 14-A)
  4. **What is the penalty for late SSS contribution remittance?** Employers face a 3% per month penalty compounded on the outstanding amount from the due date. Additionally, RA 11199 Section 28 imposes criminal liability on corporate officers: 6 years and 1 day to 12 years imprisonment plus P5,000-P20,000 fine. (RA 11199 Sec. 22, 28)
  5. **Who qualifies for SSS unemployment benefit?** You must be under 60, have at least 36 total monthly contributions with 12 in the 18 months before involuntary separation (not resignation), and file within 1 year of separation. The benefit is 50% of AMSC times 2 months, claimable once every 3 years. (RA 11199 Sec. 14-B)
  6. **What is the SSS death benefit for survivors?** If the deceased member had 36+ contributions, primary beneficiaries (legal spouse and dependent children) receive the monthly pension computed using the same three-formula BMP system. If under 36 contributions, beneficiaries receive a lump sum. Dependent children receive an additional 10% of BMP or P250 per child, up to 5 children. (RA 11199 Sec. 13)
  7. **How do I compute my SSS sickness benefit?** The daily sickness allowance is 90% of your Average Daily Salary Credit (ADSC), computed as the sum of your 6 highest Monthly Salary Credits in the 12 months before the semester of sickness, divided by 180. Maximum 120 compensable days per calendar year. You need at least 3 contributions in the 12 months before the semester of sickness. (RA 11199 Sec. 14)
  8. **What is the difference between SSS Option 1 and Option 2 for retirement?** Option 1 provides a lump sum equal to 60 months of BMP at retirement, with monthly pension resuming after 5 years. Option 2 provides 18 months of BMP as a cash payment at retirement plus immediate lifetime monthly pension. The choice is irreversible and depends on your age, life expectancy, and investment alternatives.
