# Skills & Vocational (TESDA) — angkin.ph SEO Spec

## Hub Page
- **URL:** /tesda/
- **H1:** "Skills & Vocational Certification Calculators & Tools — Philippines"
- **Overview:**

The Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA) regulates and administers the Philippines' technical-vocational education and training (TVET) system, covering over 230 qualifications across 18 sectors. In 2023, TESDA processed 935,978 competency assessments with a 93% pass rate (872,016 certified), graduated 1,428,724 TVET students from 1,633,393 enrollees, and maintained an accumulated active National Certificate (NC) base of approximately 4-5 million workers. Every NC is valid for exactly 5 years with no grace period — expiry means immediate loss of certified status, discovered only when presenting credentials to an employer or overseas recruiter.

The angkin.ph TESDA hub provides free tools that address the four primary compliance and planning needs of Philippine TVET workers: tracking NC expiry dates (with no existing TESDA notification system), computing assessment fees across 230+ qualifications (fee range of PHP 400-3,723, a 9x spread available only in scattered PDF schedules), navigating four overlapping scholarship programs (TWSP, PESFA, STEP, UAQTEA) with different eligibility rules, and helping TVET institutions manage UTPRAS registration compliance.

Unlike PRC licensure or MARINA certification, the TESDA ecosystem notably lacks a professional intermediary class — no specialist charges recurring fees to navigate TESDA certification. The primary value proposition is information aggregation and eligibility matching for the approximately 40 million working-age Filipinos and returning OFWs who could potentially qualify for TESDA skills certification. With 200,000-350,000 annual scholarship beneficiaries and a TWSP budget exceeding PHP 1 billion, the scholarship eligibility tools alone serve a massive underserved audience.

- **Hub FAQs:**

1. **How long is a TESDA National Certificate valid?** Every NC and Certificate of Competency (COC) is valid for exactly 5 years from date of issuance, per TESDA Board Resolution under RA 7796. There is no grace period — once expired, you must pass the competency assessment again to regain certified status. TESDA does not send expiry notifications.

2. **How much does a TESDA assessment cost?** Assessment fees are set by TESDA Board Resolution and range from PHP 400 (Bread & Pastry Production NC II) to PHP 3,723 (SMAW NC III welding). A flat PHP 35 processing fee is collected by the TESDA Provincial/District Office on top of the assessment fee. Private accredited assessment centers may add overhead of PHP 500-1,500 for materials and facilities.

3. **What TESDA scholarships are available and how do I qualify?** Four main programs: TWSP (age >=18, Filipino, high school/ALS grad, not enrolled in other government scholarship — no income requirement); PESFA (age >=15, Filipino, >=10 years basic education, annual family income <=PHP 300,000); STEP (short-term community-based programs); and UAQTEA (under RA 10931, targeting quality accessible education). Scholars receive PHP 160/day Training Support Fund.

4. **What is the Training Support Fund (TSF) and how much will I receive?** TSF is PHP 160 per training day, disbursed in two tranches: 50% after the first 2 weeks of training, and 50% when you reach 80% of total training duration. For a 480-hour course (60 days), total TSF = PHP 9,600. PESFA scholars also receive a PHP 2,000 book allowance (bundled programs) or PHP 500 (non-bundled), plus PHP 60/day student allowance.

5. **How do I check if my NC has expired?** You can search the TESDA Online Registry of Certified Workers by name, certificate number, or qualification. However, TESDA does not proactively send expiry reminders. You should track your issuance date and add 5 years to know your exact expiry date.

6. **What happens if my TESDA NC expires?** You lose your certified status immediately. There is no surcharge or penalty like PRC or LTO — you simply must retake and pass the competency assessment again, paying the full assessment fee. Expired NCs discovered at job applications or OFW deployment checkpoints cause immediate income risk.

7. **Can I use TWSP and PESFA at the same time?** No. TWSP eligibility requires that you are not currently a beneficiary of another government educational scholarship or subsidy. You must choose one program per training enrollment.

8. **What penalties apply if a TVET institution operates without TESDA registration?** Under the Education Act of 1982, Section 28, operating an unregistered TVET program carries a fine of PHP 2,000-10,000 plus up to 2 years imprisonment. UTPRAS registration costs PHP 2,000 per program (PHP 1,000 application + PHP 1,000 registration).
