# OFW & Overseas Workers — angkin.ph SEO Spec

## Hub Page

- **URL:** /ofw/
- **H1:** "OFW & Overseas Workers Calculators & Tools — Philippines"
- **Overview:**

The Philippines deploys 2.6 million Overseas Filipino Workers per year (2,613,903 in 2023 per DMW revised final figures), making it one of the largest labor-exporting nations on earth. Every deployment triggers a cascade of government-mandated fees, documentary requirements, and benefit entitlements governed by RA 10022 (Migrant Workers Act), RA 11641 (Department of Migrant Workers Act), and RA 10801 (OWWA Act). Yet no single authoritative tool exists to help OFWs navigate the financial and regulatory landscape of their deployment.

The angkin.ph OFW hub provides six free calculators covering the entire OFW lifecycle: from verifying whether a recruitment agency's placement fee is legal (E-OFW-1), through computing the total pre-departure budget (E-OFW-2), generating a personalized documentary checklist (E-OFW-4), to navigating post-deployment OWWA benefits including death/disability, scholarships, and the rebate program (E-OFW-3, E-OFW-5, E-OFW-6). These tools directly address documented illegal fee overcharging of PHP 45,000 to PHP 200,000 per worker, a fraud epidemic that generated 70,000+ fake job posting takedowns in 2024 alone.

The OFW population of 2.19 million currently abroad (PSA SOF 2024) represents enormous emotional and financial stakes. OFW remittances totaled USD 38.34 billion in 2024 (BSP personal remittances, record high). Every tool in this hub is backed by specific statutory sections, making the legal basis transparent and verifiable for the workers who need it most.

- **Hub FAQs:**

1. **What is the maximum placement fee a recruitment agency can legally charge an OFW?**
Under RA 10022 Section 23(b)(i) and DMW-DC-01-2023 Section 49, the maximum placement fee is one (1) month's basic salary as specified in the approved employment contract. Household service workers (HSWs), seafarers, direct hires, and workers bound for no-fee countries (UAE, Qatar, UK, Canada, etc.) must pay zero placement fee.

2. **Which countries are on the DMW no-placement-fee list?**
As of 2026, the no-fee country list includes: United Kingdom, Austria, Netherlands, Northern Ireland, Republic of Ireland, Canada (Alberta, BC, Manitoba, Saskatchewan), United States (including Guam), United Arab Emirates, and Qatar (added per DMW Advisory No. 24, Series of 2024). DMW continues to expand this list through bilateral MOUs.

3. **How much does it cost to deploy as an OFW for the first time?**
Legitimate pre-departure costs for a first-time OFW (excluding placement fee) total approximately PHP 4,000 to PHP 6,000: OWWA membership (USD 25 / ~PHP 1,450), OFW Pass/OEC (PHP 100), medical examination (PHP 2,000-3,000), PDOS registration (PHP 400), NBI clearance (PHP 115), and PSA birth certificate authentication (PHP 330). PEOS and PDOS seminars are free.

4. **What OWWA benefits am I entitled to as an active member?**
Active OWWA members are entitled to: death benefit (PHP 100,000 natural / PHP 200,000 accidental + PHP 20,000 burial), disability benefit (PHP 2,500-PHP 100,000 by impediment grade), medical assistance/MEDplus (up to PHP 50,000), educational scholarships for dependents (PHP 5,000-PHP 100,000/year depending on program), livelihood/reintegration grants (PHP 5,000-PHP 20,000), and the rebate program for long-term members.

5. **What is illegal recruitment under Philippine law?**
Under RA 10022 Section 6, illegal recruitment includes charging any amount greater than the schedule of allowable fees prescribed by DMW. Penalties are severe: 12 years + 1 day to 20 years imprisonment plus PHP 1M-PHP 2M fine for simple illegal recruitment; life imprisonment plus PHP 2M-PHP 5M fine if it constitutes economic sabotage (3 or more victims).

6. **How do I know if my recruitment agency is legitimate?**
Check the DMW's list of licensed recruitment agencies. Licensed land-based agencies pay PHP 100,000 license fee and maintain a PHP 2M escrow deposit; sea-based agencies pay PHP 200,000 and maintain PHP 5M escrow. There are approximately 1,500-2,000 active licensed agencies in the Philippines.

7. **Can my employer charge me for airfare and visa fees?**
No. Under DMW-DC-01-2023 Sections 53-54, airfare, visa fees, work permit fees, and agency service fees are exclusively chargeable to the employer/principal, not the OFW. The RA 10022 Section 37-A compulsory insurance (USD 15,000 death + USD 10,000 repatriation + USD 7,500 disability) is also borne by the recruitment agency/principal.

8. **What is the OWWA rebate program and am I eligible?**
The OWWA rebate program (MC No. 003 S. 2020) pays eligible members a rebate computed as: (Number of contributions minus 5) x PHP 100 x Loyalty Multiplier (1.0 for <15 years, 1.1 for 15-19 years, 1.2 for 20+ years). Eligibility requires minimum 10 years of OWWA membership, at least 5 contributions as of December 31, 2017, and no prior OWWA benefit claims. 556,000 members were identified as eligible in the first batch (PHP 1B allocation).
