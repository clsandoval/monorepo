- **URL:** /data-privacy/

- **Overview:**

The Philippines' Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) imposes a multi-layered compliance framework on every organization that processes personal data of Filipinos. Enforced by the National Privacy Commission (NPC), the law carries administrative penalties up to P5 million and criminal penalties including up to 7 years imprisonment for violations such as unauthorized processing, concealment of security breaches, and malicious disclosure. The compliance universe is vast: banks, hospitals, schools, BPOs, retailers, insurance companies, and government agencies all process personal data at scale.

Despite the breadth of coverage, actual compliance rates remain very low. NPC Circular No. 2022-04 made registration mandatory for organizations employing 250 or more persons, processing sensitive personal information of 1,000 or more individuals, or conducting processing likely to pose a risk to data subjects — yet NPC had to introduce the Sworn Declaration and Undertaking (SDAU) exemption in October 2024 to handle the flood of non-compliant entities. An estimated 15,000 to 30,000 organizations are mandatory registrants, while 50,000 to 100,000 organizations must file the Annual Security Incident Report (ASIR) regardless of registration status. Many SMEs — pharmacies, clinics, dental offices, school registrars, HR departments — process 1,000+ sensitive personal information records unknowingly and should be registered but have never been.

These tools help organizations determine their NPC registration obligations, manage the time-critical 72-hour breach notification protocol (where concealment carries 18 months to 5 years imprisonment), prepare and file the annual ASIR by the March 31 deadline, and screen whether their data processing activities trigger a mandatory Privacy Impact Assessment. Together, they replace the most mechanical portions of data privacy compliance consulting — the threshold checks, calendar tracking, and notification checklists — that law firms and consultants currently charge P50,000 to P500,000 per engagement to provide.

- **Hub FAQs:**

1. **Who must register with the NPC?** Under NPC Circular No. 2022-04 (effective January 11, 2023), mandatory registration is triggered if your organization meets ANY of three conditions: (a) employs 250 or more persons, (b) processes sensitive personal information (SPI) of 1,000 or more individuals, or (c) processes data that will likely pose a risk to the rights and freedoms of data subjects. SPI includes race/ethnicity, health/medical, religious beliefs, sexual life, government-issued IDs, financial/credit records, and biometric data.

2. **What is the 72-hour breach notification requirement?** Under RA 10173 Sec. 20(f) and NPC Circular 16-03, organizations must notify both the NPC AND all affected data subjects within 72 hours of discovering a personal data breach that is likely to result in harm. The 72-hour clock starts immediately upon "knowledge" of the breach. A full breach report must follow within 5 days of the initial notification.

3. **What are the penalties for concealing a data breach?** Under RA 10173 Sec. 30, concealment of a security breach involving sensitive personal information carries imprisonment of 18 months to 5 years AND a fine of P500,000 to P1,000,000. When 100 or more data subjects are affected, maximum penalties apply under Sec. 35.

4. **What is the ASIR and who must file it?** The Annual Security Incident Report is required under NPC Circular 16-03 Sec. 7 for ALL personal information controllers (PICs) and personal information processors (PIPs) in the Philippines, regardless of NPC registration status. It must be filed via the DBNMS platform between January 1 and March 31 each year. Even organizations with zero security incidents must file a "zero-incident" report. Non-filing carries up to P50,000 administrative fine.

5. **What is a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) and when is it mandatory?** PIAs are mandatory for all government agencies processing personal data (NPC Circular 2016-01) and triggered for private sector organizations when processing involves large scale (1,000+ individuals), sensitive categories, automated decision-making, cross-border transfers, or new technologies. NPC Circular 2023-06 (effective March 30, 2024) added new minimum security requirements with a March 30, 2025 compliance deadline.

6. **How much do data privacy consultants charge in the Philippines?** Full compliance program setup (Privacy Management Program development + PIA + DPO training + NPC registration): P50,000-P500,000 per engagement. Outsourced DPO retainer: P20,000-P100,000/month. Annual compliance management including ASIR preparation: P30,000-P150,000/year. Incident response retainer: P100,000-P500,000 per engagement.

7. **What is the DBNMS?** The Data Breach Notification Management System (dbnms.privacy.gov.ph) is the NPC's mandatory online platform for breach notification submissions and ASIR filing. Paper and email submissions are not accepted. Organizations must have an active DBNMS account to comply with breach notification and ASIR requirements.

8. **What is the NPC Sworn Declaration and Undertaking (SDAU)?** Implemented October 2024 under NPC Circular 2023-01, the SDAU allows organizations that do NOT meet any of the three mandatory registration thresholds to submit a declaration of exemption instead of full DPS registration. This was introduced to manage the volume of non-compliant entities who incorrectly believed they needed to register.
