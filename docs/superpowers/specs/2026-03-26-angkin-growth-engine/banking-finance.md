## Hub Page

- **URL:** /banking/
- **H1:** "Banking, Finance & Gaming Calculators & Tools — Philippines"
- **Overview:**

The Philippine financial services and gaming sectors operate under some of the most complex and rapidly evolving regulatory frameworks in Southeast Asia. The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) supervises over 31,000 pawnshop and money service business offices nationwide, while PAGCOR oversees a gaming industry that posted an all-time record P372.33 billion in gross gaming revenue for 2024 — a 30.5% increase year-over-year.

BSP Circular No. 1206 (December 2024) consolidated all MSB rules into a single framework, creating immediate compliance uncertainty for existing operators reclassifying under new type labels. Meanwhile, PAGCOR's license fee rates changed three times in 30 months (50-55% in 2022 to 30%/25% in January 2025), and a new Minimum Guaranteed Fee floor of P9 million/month takes effect April 2026. Both sectors carry severe AML penalties: P500,000-P1,000,000 per missed CTR filing under RA 9160, and up to P50 million in civil penalties for gaming operators under RA 10927.

These tools help pawnshop borrowers verify loan costs against PD 114 rate caps, assist the 2,151 BSP-regulated entities with supervision fee projections and AMLA compliance calendars, enable fintech founders to determine their correct BSP license classification and capital requirements, and give PAGCOR-licensed gaming operators the fee dashboards, AML trackers, and tax classification tools they need to stay compliant in a high-enforcement environment.

- **Hub FAQs:**

1. **What is a BSP-supervised non-bank financial institution?** BSP supervises pawnshops (16,219 offices), remittance transfer companies, money changers/FX dealers, e-money issuers (EMIs), virtual asset service providers (VASPs), and operators of payment systems (OPS) — collectively over 31,000 offices/agents nationwide under RA 7653, RA 11211, RA 11127, and PD 114.

2. **What changed with BSP Circular No. 1206 (December 2024)?** Circular 1206 consolidated all MSB rules into the new "M-Regulations" in the MORNBFI, unifying RTCs, MC/FXDs, EMIs, and VASPs under a single regulatory framework. Existing operators must reclassify under new type labels (A, B, D, E, F) and verify their compliance obligations under the new structure.

3. **What are the AMLA penalties for pawnshops and MSBs?** Under RA 9160 Sec. 14, failure to file a Covered Transaction Report (CTR) for transactions of P500,000 or more, or a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR), carries penalties of P500,000 to P1,000,000 per violation plus imprisonment. Pawnshops are classified as covered institutions under AMLC Registration and Reporting Guidelines (2021).

4. **How does PAGCOR's franchise tax work?** Under PD 1869 Sec. 13(2)(a), gaming income is subject to a 5% franchise tax on GGR in lieu of ALL other taxes (income tax, VAT, local taxes). Non-gaming income (F&B, hotel, entertainment, retail) is subject to regular 25% CIT + 12% VAT. RMC 132-2024 clarified that this benefit extends to PAGCOR's contractees and licensees.

5. **What is the PAGCOR Minimum Guaranteed Fee (MGF)?** Effective April 1, 2026, e-casino operators must pay a minimum of P9,000,000/month regardless of actual GGR. The benchmark GGR is P30,000,000/month — operators falling below this threshold still owe the full MGF. This creates a cliff-edge compliance risk for smaller operators.

6. **What is the casino AML/CTR threshold?** Under RA 10927 and the Casino Implementing Rules and Regulations (CIRR, 2017), a Covered Transaction Report must be filed when a single transaction or aggregate transactions within one gaming day reach P5,000,000 or more per patron. STRs have no minimum threshold.

7. **How much capital do I need for a BSP EMI license?** Large-scale EMIs (12-month average transaction volume >= P25 billion) require P200 million minimum capital. Small-scale EMIs (below P25 billion) require P100 million. Additionally, EMIs must maintain liquidity of at least 50% of outstanding e-money balance (if >= P100 million) in trust accounts, per BSP Circular No. 1166 (2023).

8. **Who needs to register with PAGCOR?** All casino operators, e-games/e-bingo operators, bingo operations, sports betting operators, junket operators, and gaming service providers must be accredited with PAGCOR. The number of accredited gaming service providers grew 3x from 49 (2023) to 174 (2024).
