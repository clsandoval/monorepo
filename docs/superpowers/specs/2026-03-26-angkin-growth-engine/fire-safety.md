## Hub Page
- **URL:** /fire-safety/
- **H1:** "Fire Safety Compliance Calculators & Tools — Philippines"
- **Overview:**

The Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) is the primary agency responsible for fire prevention, suppression, and investigation in the Philippines. Under Republic Act No. 9514 (Fire Code of the Philippines of 2008) and its Revised IRR (2019 edition), all commercial, industrial, educational, residential (multi-family), healthcare, and entertainment establishments must obtain fire safety certificates before commencing or continuing operations. Two primary certificates gate the building lifecycle: the Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) for new construction and the Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) for annual business permit renewal.

With 1.2M+ registered business establishments, approximately 3.2M annual BFP inspections, and a 5.3% non-compliance rate resulting in roughly 170,000 establishments failing inspection each year (leading to immediate operations stoppage via Notice to Close/Stop Operations), fire safety compliance is one of the highest-frequency and highest-stakes regulatory touchpoints for Philippine businesses. BFP collected approximately PHP 5.5B+ annually in fire code fees.

These tools address the two core pain points: (1) not knowing what fire safety equipment and systems your building requires before the inspector arrives, and (2) missing the annual January 20 compliance deadline crunch. The BFP's own e-BFP portal (fsis.e-bfp.com) handles transaction processing but does not offer compliance screening or fee calculation.

- **How It Works Content:**
  - FSIC fee = 10% of all fees charged by the BPLO/Building Official for the business permit, occupancy permit, or annual inspection (RA 9514 Sec. 13; IRR Rule 12.0), with a floor of PHP 500

  - Some regions (e.g., Region 9) apply 15% per updated Citizens' Charters rather than the statutory 10% — creating confusion for multi-region businesses
