- **Overview:**

Philippine labor law imposes dozens of deterministic computation obligations on every private-sector employer — from daily payroll premiums that compound across holiday type, overtime, and night shift factors, to mandatory annual benefits like the 13th month pay, to final pay computations that must be released within 30 days of separation under DOLE Labor Advisory 06-20. The Labor Code (PD 442), its implementing rules, and special statutes like RA 7641 (Retirement Pay Law) and PD 851 (13th Month Pay) define exact formulas that employers and employees routinely get wrong, generating NLRC disputes, DOLE violations, and systematic underpayment.

The angkin.ph Labor & Employment hub provides nine free, statute-grounded calculator tools covering the full lifecycle of Philippine employment compensation — from daily payroll rate verification to separation, retirement, and back wages estimation. Each tool applies the exact statutory formulas defined by the Labor Code, DOLE issuances, and Supreme Court jurisprudence, replacing the ₱3,000–₱50,000 professional consultations that currently gatekeep what is fundamentally arithmetic.

With approximately 24.9 million private-sector wage workers, 1.1 million SSS-registered employers, and hundreds of thousands of annual employment separations, these tools address the largest single compliance domain in the Philippine regulatory landscape. Whether you are an HR officer computing final pay for a resigned employee, a retiring factory worker verifying your RA 7641 entitlement, or an employer checking your holiday pay rate matrix against DOLE standards, every tool here computes exactly what the law requires — no lawyer needed.

- **Hub FAQs:**
  1. "What is included in final pay in the Philippines?" — Unpaid wages, pro-rated 13th month pay, cash conversion of unused SIL, separation pay (if applicable), and any other unpaid benefits per DOLE LA 06-20.
  2. "How many days does an employer have to release final pay?" — 30 days from date of separation, per DOLE Labor Advisory No. 06-20 (2020).
  3. "How is retirement pay computed under RA 7641?" — Daily rate (monthly salary / 26) x 22.5 days x years of service. The 22.5 days includes 15 days salary + 5 days SIL + 1/12 of 13th month pay.
  4. "What is the holiday pay rate for working on a regular holiday in the Philippines?" — 200% of the daily rate for the first 8 hours; 260% for overtime hours; add 10% for night shift differential (10PM-6AM).
  5. "How is 13th month pay calculated for employees who resigned mid-year?" — Total basic salary earned from January 1 (or hire date) to last day of work, divided by 12.
  6. "What is the separation pay formula for redundancy vs. retrenchment?" — Redundancy: 1 month salary per year of service. Retrenchment: 1/2 month salary per year of service, or 1 month salary, whichever is higher.
  7. "How do I compute SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG contributions?" — SSS uses a 31-bracket monthly salary credit table (15% total in 2025); PhilHealth is 5% of monthly basic salary split equally; Pag-IBIG is 1-2% employee + 2% employer on salary up to P5,000 base.
  8. "What is the current minimum wage in NCR?" — P695/day for non-agricultural workers; P658/day for agricultural and small establishments, per NCR Wage Order No. 26 effective July 18, 2025.
