## Hub Page

- **URL:** /property/
- **H1:** "Property & Real Estate Calculators & Tools — Philippines"
- **Overview:**

Philippine real property is governed by a dense stack of overlapping statutes — the Local Government Code (RA 7160), the RPVARA (RA 12001), the Maceda Law (RA 6552), and BIR regulations on capital gains and transfer taxes. Every property owner, buyer, seller, and developer must navigate computations that span multiple agencies (LGU Treasurer, BIR, Registry of Deeds) and multiple valuation bases (Fair Market Value, Zonal Value, Schedule of Market Values).

The angkin.ph Property hub covers the full lifecycle of Philippine real property compliance: annual Real Property Tax computation, Local Business Tax for commercial properties, the time-limited RPVARA tax amnesty for delinquent accounts, and the Maceda Law protections that safeguard millions of installment buyers. Each tool automates statutory formulas that currently cost Filipino property owners thousands to tens of thousands of pesos in professional fees — or worse, cost them money they never know they are owed.

With approximately 21.6 million Filipino households owning or amortizing property (PSA 2020 CPH), 1.24 million registered businesses requiring annual permits, 2-5 million delinquent RPT accounts eligible for amnesty, and 3-5 million active installment contracts under Maceda Law, the Property hub addresses the single largest compliance surface area in the Philippines outside of income tax.
