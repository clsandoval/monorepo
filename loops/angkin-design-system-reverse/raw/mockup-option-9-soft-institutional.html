What the Law Provides

Under Republic Act 7641, private sector employees who retire at age 60 (optional) or 65 (compulsory) after at least five (5) years of service are entitled to a retirement pay of:

½ month salary × years of service, with a fraction of at least 6 months counted as one whole year

The "½ month salary" means 22.5 days: 15 days basic pay + 1/12 of the 13th month pay + 5 days SIL. This is the statutory minimum; individual employment contracts may provide higher benefits.

Source: RA 7641 — The Retirement Pay Law (1992) and Department of Labor and Employment Advisory No. 06-2020.

Common Questions

  • Does this cover government employees?

    No. Government employees are covered under the GSIS Act (RA 8291). This calculator applies to private sector employees only.

  • What if my CBA provides higher benefits?

    The CBA provision applies. RA 7641 is the floor, not the ceiling. Always use the more favorable provision for the employee.

  • Is retirement pay taxable?

    Generally exempt from income tax under NIRC Section 32(B)(6)(a), provided the employee is at least 50 years old and has been employed for 10+ years with the same employer.
