About Republic Act No. 7641

RA 7641, the Retirement Pay Act, requires Philippine private sector employers to pay retiring employees a minimum benefit of one-half month salary for every year of service (equivalent to 22.5 days). This applies when the employer has no existing retirement plan, or when the company plan provides less than the legal minimum.

Under DOLE guidelines, "one-half month salary" means: 15 days salary + 1/12 of 13th month pay + 5 days service incentive leave — totaling 22.5 days when added together. The benefit applies to all employees except those covered by the Government Service Insurance System (GSIS).
