Computation Breakdown
Source: Republic Act No. 7641 (1992), amending Art. 287 of the Labor Code. Retirement pay = ½ month salary × years of service. "½ month salary" includes: 15 days salary + 1/12 of 13th month pay + 5 days SIL (if applicable). Fraction ≥ 6 months counts as one year.
