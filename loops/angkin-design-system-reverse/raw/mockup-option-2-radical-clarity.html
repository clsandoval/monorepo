Kalkulador ng Retirement Pay Retirement Pay Calculator (RA 7641)

Batayan sa batas · Legal basis

Republic Act No. 7641 — Retirement Pay Law — nagbibigay ng karapatan sa mga empleyadong pribadong sektor na makatanggap ng retirement pay pagkatapos ng 5 taon ng serbisyo at sa edad na 60 pataas.

Private sector employees who have served at least 5 years are entitled to retirement pay equivalent to at least 1/2 month salary per year of service.

Huwag isama ang overtime, allowances, o bonuses — basic pay lang (Do not include overtime, allowances, or bonuses — basic pay only)
