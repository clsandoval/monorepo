Key Rules

Minimum Qualification 60 years old · At least 5 years of service
Fraction of Year 6+ months = 1 full year for computation
Higher Benefit Prevails Company/CBA benefit vs. RA 7641 — employee gets the higher amount
Tax Exemption Retirement pay under RA 7641 is generally tax-exempt under NIRC Sec. 32(B)(6)(b)
