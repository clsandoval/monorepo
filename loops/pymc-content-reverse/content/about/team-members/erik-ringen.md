# Erik Ringen

## Role
Principal Data Scientist; Biological Anthropologist; Bayesian Modeler

## Bio
Erik J. Ringen, PhD, is a biological anthropologist and data scientist whose work bridges evolutionary behavioral science and applied Bayesian modeling. He completed his PhD at Emory University (Jaeggi Lab), where his dissertation focused on phylogenetic comparative methods using Stan. Since 2022, he has been a Data Scientist at LiRI (Linguistic Research Infrastructure, University of Zurich), supporting NCCR researchers with statistical methods and teaching probabilistic modelling to graduate students. At PyMC Labs, he specializes in causality, marketing mix modeling, and teaching Bayesian methods. He has 20+ publications with 238–485+ citations and won the H. Russell Bernard Graduate Student Paper Prize (American Anthropological Association, 2018).

## Education
