# Kemble Fletcher

## Role
Advisor (formerly co-founder of SweepLift)

## Bio
Before joining PyMC Labs, Kemble co-founded SweepLift, where he co-invented a patent-pending in-stream survey and measurement technology, leading product development and Bayesian modeling initiatives that fueled exponential client growth. He previously served as a senior leader at Google, overseeing omnichannel attribution and measurement strategy for the company's top 300 global clients, directly influencing $2B in annual recurring revenue and pioneering privacy-forward measurement approaches. Earlier career work at OMD involved advancing digital analytics and predictive modeling for brands including Levi's, Dockers, Hilton Hotels, and eHarmony. Currently advises SaaS executives and startups on data architecture, marketing attribution, and growth strategies while leveraging probabilistic modeling to solve complex business challenges.
