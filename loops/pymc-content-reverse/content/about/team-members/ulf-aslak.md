# Ulf Aslak

## Role
Data Scientist and Physicist

## Bio
Ulf is a data scientist and physicist with a PhD in complex systems. He specializes in Artificial Intelligence and Bayesian modeling for marketing measurement—particularly MMM—and has deep experience connecting advanced statistics with production-ready software. His professional background includes academic research, where he contributed to publications in premier journals like Nature, plus industry work building and deploying marketing analytics solutions for established brands. He pairs mathematical depth with strong engineering capabilities.
