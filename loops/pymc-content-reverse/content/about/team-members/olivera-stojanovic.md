# Olivera Stojanovic

## Role
Data Scientist

## Bio
Olivera is a data scientist with nine years of experience in Bayesian inference, forecasting, and predictive modeling. She has applied these methods in public health, environmental science, and the analysis of labor market and economic data. Previously worked at Michael Bauer Research focusing on predictive modeling for labor market indicators and collaborated with Data Science Alliance on food demand forecasting for food banks.

## Education
PhD in Cognitive Science, Osnabrück University; MSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Novi Sad; BSc in Medical Physics, University of Novi Sad

## Career
Michael Bauer Research (predictive modeling for labor market indicators); Data Science Alliance (food demand forecasting for food banks); Data Scientist at PyMC Labs

## Specializations
Blogging/Webinars, Causality, Machine Learning, Modeling, Project Management, Time-Series
