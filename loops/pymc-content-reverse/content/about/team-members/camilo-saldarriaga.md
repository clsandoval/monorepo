# Camilo Saldarriaga

## Role
Financial Economist / Consultant

## Bio
A financial economist with strong expertise in Bayesian statistical modeling, asset pricing, and financial econometrics. Experience in financial modeling for investment funds and has worked as a consultant in statistical modeling for the OECD. Career includes a position in the Office of the President of Costa Rica, where he was part of the negotiation team for the country's Stand-By Agreement with the International Monetary Fund.

## Education
Master's in Economics, École d'Économie de la Sorbonne; Master's in Financial Economics, École d'Économie de la Sorbonne; Doctoral Candidate in Finance, ESCP Business School

## Career
Consultant in statistical modeling for the OECD; negotiation team member at Office of the President of Costa Rica (IMF Stand-By Agreement)

## Specializations
Asset Pricing, Coding, Data Science, Finance, Modeling, Statistical Analysis, Time-Series, Macroeconomics
