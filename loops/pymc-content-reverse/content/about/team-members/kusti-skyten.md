# Kusti Skyten

## Role
Associate Data Scientist

## Bio
Kusti Skytén is an Associate Data Scientist at PyMC Labs with expertise in mathematics and Bayesian statistics. His work centers on integrating generative AI into the Bayesian workflow to enhance model development and streamline validation to deliver uncertainty-aware insights. Previously at the University of Oslo, he conducted research using simulator-based inference and Bayesian optimization for modeling bacterial population dynamics in vaccine optimization work.

## Education
Master's degree in Mathematical Statistics, University of Cambridge; research at University of Oslo

## Career
Research at University of Oslo (simulator-based inference, Bayesian optimization, bacterial population dynamics, vaccine optimization); Associate Data Scientist at PyMC Labs

## Specializations
Coding, GP, GenAI, ML, MLOps, MMM, Math, Modeling, ODEs, Package management, Solvers/Optimizers, Statistical Analysis, Teaching
