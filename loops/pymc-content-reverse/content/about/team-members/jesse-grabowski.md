# Jesse Grabowski

## Role
Principal Data Scientist; PyMC/PyTensor Core Developer; State Space Modeling specialist

## Bio
Jesse Byron Grabowski is a Principal Data Scientist at PyMC Labs and a core developer of PyMC and PyTensor. He is a PhD candidate in Economics (Macroeconomics, Finance, Development) at the University of Paris I: Panthéon-Sorbonne. He has a diverse background spanning international development macroeconomics at the OECD, Peace Corps agricultural work, and teaching. He is the author of `pymc_statespace`, a Bayesian state space modeling library (now integrated into pymc-extras), and is a Google Summer of Code 2024 mentor for probabilistic modeling extensions in PyMC.
