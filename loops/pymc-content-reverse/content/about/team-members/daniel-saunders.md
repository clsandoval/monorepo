# Daniel Saunders

## Role
Data Scientist

## Bio
Daniel is an expert in building fast and stable Bayesian models for pricing and marketing applications. He holds a PhD from the University of British Columbia where he did research in evolutionary game theory. He taught scientific computing, statistics, and modeling in the Cognitive Systems program at UBC for several years.
