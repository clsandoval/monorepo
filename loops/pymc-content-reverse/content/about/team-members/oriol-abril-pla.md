# Oriol Abril Pla

## Role
Core contributor to ArviZ and PyMC

## Bio
Oriol discovered his passion for computational statistics and open source in 2018 during his MSc in Astrophysics. He began contributing to ArviZ and PyMC in 2019, later joining their core teams. He transitioned from academia to work more freely on open source, software development, and knowledge sharing. His primary interests include data visualization, model diagnostics, inference diagnostics, model comparison, and prior elicitation. Within open source projects, he has contributed substantially to documentation, governance, and EDIA (Equity, Diversity, Inclusion, and Accessibility) initiatives.
