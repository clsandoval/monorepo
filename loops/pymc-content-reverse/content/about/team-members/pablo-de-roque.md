# Pablo de Roque

## Role
Sr Data Scientist at Glovo; contributor to PyMC Labs

## Bio
Pablo brings expertise from delivery logistics and financial services sectors. His professional background spans consulting firms (Accenture, KPMG) and experimental physics. He specializes in production-grade data science solutions including product analytics, causal inference, fraud detection, time estimation, route optimization, and uplift modeling. He develops marketing mix modeling and customer lifetime value systems, with contributions to the pymc-marketing library. His technical skillset encompasses MLOps tools (Argo, Kafka, Github Actions), observability platforms (DataDog, Opentelemetry, Grafana), and systems design.
