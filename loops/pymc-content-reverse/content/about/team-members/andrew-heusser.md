# Andrew Heusser

## Role
Data Scientist / Contractor (operates Cognitive Insight Consulting, LLC)

## Bio
A data scientist with 16 years of experience, spanning academic memory modeling to leading Akili Interactive's data science team. At Akili Interactive, constructed machine learning product features, executed Bayesian marketing mix models via PyMC-Marketing, and managed rapid A/B experimentation. Currently operates Cognitive Insight Consulting, LLC, concentrating on agentic applications, digital health solutions, and Bayesian/ML modeling while subcontracting with PyMC Labs.
