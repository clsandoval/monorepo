# Allen Downey

## Role
Principal Data Scientist; Professor Emeritus at Olin College

## Bio
A Professor Emeritus at Olin College and Principal Data Scientist at PyMC Labs. Author of Think Python, Think Stats, and Probably Overthinking It, plus a blog about programming and data science. A consultant and instructor specializing in Bayesian statistics.

## Education
PhD in Computer Science, University of California Berkeley; Master's and Bachelor's degrees from MIT

## Career
Professor Emeritus at Olin College; consultant and instructor

## Specializations
Accounting, Blogging/Webinars, Coding, Data Science, Data Visualization, GenAI, Modeling, Project Management, Statistical Analysis, Survey Measurement, Survival Analysis, Teaching, Time-Series
