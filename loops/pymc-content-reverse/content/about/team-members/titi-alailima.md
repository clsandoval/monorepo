# Titi Alailima

## Role
Data Scientist and Software Engineer

## Bio
Titi Ala'ilima is a data scientist and software engineer with a passion for improving the world through technology. Originally trained as an electrical engineer, he transitioned to software during the dotcom era and has maintained expertise across the full technology stack as it evolved. He emphasizes the importance of data architecture in modern systems. He champions a "SCALE ethos: Spread a Culture of Accelerated Learning through Experimentation," encouraging teams to adopt this philosophy. Most recently at Akili, he demonstrated capability in mastering diverse domains including medical device regulation, computational neuroscience, and Marketing Mix Modeling. Regarding AI applications, he advocates that "AI raises the floor so that humans may raise the ceiling!" Outside of work, he enjoys gaming, puzzles, and crafting cocktails. His autistic son has inspired him to explore supporting neurodiverse individuals through technology.
