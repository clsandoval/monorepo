# Benjamin Maier

## Role
Principal Data Scientist; Computational Epidemiologist; Physicist

## Bio
Benjamin F. Maier is a physicist and data scientist with a PhD in Theoretical Physics from Humboldt University Berlin. His research sits at the intersection of complex networks, epidemic modeling, and applied machine learning. He has 30+ published articles with 1,900+ citations (h-index 12), including publications in *Science* and *PNAS*. He advised the German government on COVID-19 model-informed mitigation strategies and collaborated with the Robert Koch Institute, the Technical University of Denmark, Santa Fe Institute, Deutsche Bank, and Universal Music Group. He is an affiliate researcher at SynoSys (Center for Interdisciplinary Digital Science, TU Dresden) and maintains open-source tools across PyMC and conda-forge.
