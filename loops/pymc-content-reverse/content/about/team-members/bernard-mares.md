# Bernard Mares

## Role
Independent Mathematician & Data Science Engineer; frequent PyMC Labs collaborator

## Bio
A France-based independent mathematician, data science engineer, and frequent PyMC Labs collaborator. Builds Bayesian models and the production systems that run them: clean pipelines, reproducible environments, and reliable deployments. Bernard holds a PhD in mathematical physics from MIT and maintains various open-source projects across PyMC and conda-forge.

## Education
