# Ben Vincent

## Role
Senior Data Scientist; Course Instructor; CausalPy creator; pymc-marketing contributor

## Bio
Benjamin T. Vincent, DPhil, is a UK-based Senior Data Scientist at PyMC Labs with a background in neuroscience and computational modelling. He spent 15 years as a Lecturer at the University of Dundee researching decision making, delay discounting, Bayesian inference, and attention. He is the creator of **CausalPy**, PyMC Labs' open-source package for Bayesian causal inference in quasi-experimental settings (synthetic control, interrupted time series, difference in differences, regression discontinuity). He also runs InferenceWorks Ltd, a consulting company. He has 1,549+ citations on Google Scholar and has worked with clients including Alva Labs, Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, Colgate-Palmolive, Gain Theory, and HelloFresh.
