# Embedded Teams — PyMC Labs

## Page Purpose

Embedded Teams is the **"We Work By Your Side"** pillar — the fourth and most hands-on service offering. PyMC Labs experts join a client's internal team (via Slack, GitHub, and direct collaboration) to co-deliver projects while naturally upskilling the client's data scientists through daily collaboration. Distinct from EAP (advisory/coaching) and Solution Delivery (PyMC Labs owns the deliverable): Embedded Teams is about working inside the client's org.

## How It Works

### The Model
- PyMC Labs experts join the client's existing Slack workspace and GitHub repositories
- Work side-by-side on active projects — not advising from the outside but contributing as part of the team
- Knowledge transfer happens naturally through daily collaboration, code review, and co-authoring models
- No context-switching: experts work inside the client's tooling and data stack

### What Gets Done
- Co-build complex Bayesian models alongside client data scientists
- Unblock stalled projects: diagnose sampling issues, improve model architecture, fix convergence problems
- Accelerate delivery of in-flight work
- Progressively hand off ownership as client team builds proficiency
- Leave behind documented, maintainable code the team can extend independently

### Key Differentiator vs. Other Offerings

| Offering | Mode | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy & Advisory (EAP) | Async + coaching calls | Guidance on your team's work |
| Solution Delivery | PyMC Labs builds it | You receive a finished deliverable |
| **Embedded Teams** | **Side-by-side collaboration** | **Faster delivery + internal capability** |
| Training & Enablement | Structured curriculum | Knowledge transfer in formal sessions |

## Value Propositions

### 1. Speed When You're Stuck

> "value prop is also speed. In-house DS get bogged down or stuck, we help them develop, troubleshoot, and implement much faster"
— Evan (UTC -5), #sales, 2024-12-04

### 2. Fractional Senior Expertise Without the Hire

Companies with in-house data science teams often hit a ceiling — they have capable generalists but lack the specialized Bayesian depth for the hardest problems. Hiring senior Bayesian practitioners is slow, expensive, and competitive.

> "Part of me thinks that we probably have a network of EAPs where they have a good in-house model but struggling to scale it. e.g Haleon is one we know for sure"
— Niall, #sales, 2025-11-24

### 3. Skills Transfer That Sticks

Unlike outsourced delivery that creates black-box dependency, Embedded Teams builds internal capability. Client data scientists learn by doing — not through abstract workshops but through real project collaboration.

> "Teaching fundamentals enables clients to diagnose and solve problems independently."
— Teemu Säilynoja, expert perspective on coaching, website-scrape/crawl-remaining.md

> "The goal involves teaching clients model fundamentals so they can troubleshoot independently while collaborating on complex problems."
— Bill Engels, expert perspective, website-scrape/crawl-remaining.md

### 4. No Loss of Context

Working directly in client Slack and GitHub means zero translation layer. Experts have full visibility into the client's codebase, data, and business context from day one.
