# Syngenta: Expert Review of Bayesian Dose-Response Models for Agrochemical Research

## Client

**Syngenta** — global agrochemicals and crop protection research company.

- **Industry:** Agriculture / Agrochemicals / Crop Protection Research
- **Service:** Strategy & Advisory (SLA / coaching format)
- **Engagement period:** SOW 1 completed March 2025; SOW 2 completed December 2025

## The Problem

Syngenta's internal data science team had built a probabilistic modeling pipeline for XC50 assay modeling — a standard method for measuring chemical potency (the concentration at which a compound achieves 50% of maximum effect). They needed expert external review of:

1. **XC50 assay modeling** using Bayesian dose-response curves
2. **Relative potency models** for comparing compound efficacy
3. **Hierarchical GLM binomial models** for desirability functions
4. **Deployment architecture** for production use in Dataiku DSS environment

This was a coaching and review engagement: PyMC Labs reviewed Syngenta's own models and provided expert feedback rather than building from scratch.

## Approach

In the SLA coaching format, PyMC Labs:

- Reviewed Syngenta's Bayesian binomial GLM models for dose-response data
- Provided guidance on hierarchical Bayesian structures for relative potency estimation
- Conducted code architecture review for production deployment on **Dataiku DSS**
- Performed R² analysis for model comparison
- Delivered iterative written and live feedback over two SOWs
