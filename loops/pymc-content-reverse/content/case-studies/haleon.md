# Haleon: Bayesian Modeling Advisory and Team Upskilling for Consumer Healthcare

## Client

**Haleon** — consumer healthcare company; brands include Sensodyne, Panadol, and Voltaren.

- **Industry:** Consumer Healthcare / Pharma
- **Service:** Strategy & Advisory (Bayesian modeling SLA — code review and coaching)
- **Contacts:** Nathan Kafi (Principal Data Scientist), Chris

## The Problem

Haleon had an internal Bayesian modeling team building hierarchical models for marketing analytics. They needed expert code audit, architectural guidance, and upskilling — not full model development. Key challenges:

1. **Code audit and architectural review** of existing Bayesian models
2. **Missing values in hierarchical models** with unbalanced panel data — a technically subtle problem in PyMC
3. **GPU acceleration** on Databricks using JAX/NumPyro sampler setup
4. **VAR model guidance** for time series applications

The SLA format was deliberately scoped to coaching and review, enabling the Haleon team to build their own sustained capability.

## Approach

- Biweekly calls for structured code review and Q&A
- Technical guidance on missing value handling in unbalanced panel hierarchical models
- JAX/NumPyro GPU setup walkthrough for Databricks environment
- VAR model architectural guidance
- Juan Orduz presented PyMC Labs to the broader Haleon analytics organization — expanding visibility beyond the immediate team

## Results

- Haleon's internal team upskilled and empowered to build their own Bayesian models
- Strong relationship opened potential for other Haleon business units
- Juan presented PyMC Labs to the entire Haleon analytics team, broadening the internal champion base
