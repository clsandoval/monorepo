### The Challenge Section

**Lead:** "AI tools are creating a new kind of risk."

**Without This Workshop (problems):**
1. ⏳ "You're still doing it the slow way" — Hours wasted on EDA, cleaning, and boilerplate that AI could handle in minutes — while your actual expertise sits idle.
2. ⚠️ "AI-generated analysis is a liability" — Models confidently hallucinate statistical conclusions. You can't catch errors you don't know to look for — until they reach a stakeholder.
3. 🌀 "Prompt-and-pray isn't a workflow" — No structure, no reproducibility, no way to scale what works across projects or hand off to a team.

**After This Workshop (outcomes):**
1. ✓ "Direct AI through the full analytics stack" — From raw data to Bayesian decision-making — spec-driven, reproducible, and defensible at every step.
2. ✓ "Catch statistical errors before they ship" — Adversarial multi-model review, confounding checks, and uncertainty quantification built into every pipeline.
3. ✓ "Build systems that compound in value" — Reusable templates and parallel workflows that make your next analysis take hours, not weeks.

---

### What You'll Learn (5 Skills)

1. **Direct AI through the full analytics ladder** — Descriptive → diagnostic → predictive → prescriptive → Bayesian, on one real business dataset, end-to-end, in hours.
2. **Plan before you execute: spec-driven development** — Create the plan, review it, then build. Eliminate aimless prompting and produce reproducible results every time.
3. **Catch the errors AI doesn't flag** — Statistical mistakes, confounding, misinterpreted outputs. Run adversarial multi-model reviews before anything reaches a stakeholder.
4. **Give stakeholders uncertainty, not false precision** — Go beyond point estimates to Bayesian decision-making. Because "the model says 42" isn't a business answer.
5. **Scale one workflow into five** — Build reusable templates, agent skills, and parallel pipelines so your next analysis takes hours — not weeks.
