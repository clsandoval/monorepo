## CASE STUDY: HelloFresh (/work/hellofresh)

**Company:** HelloFresh
**Services:** Bayesian A/B Testing, Media Mix Modeling (MMM)
**Year:** 2025
**Website:** https://www.hellofresh.com/

### Summary
"We partnered with HelloFresh to replace 'black-box' attribution with a high-performance Bayesian framework for Media Mix Modeling and A/B testing. This collaboration unlocked a 60x increase in experimentation speed and provided a transparent, data-driven map for optimizing global marketing spend."

### About Project
HelloFresh (world's leading meal-kit provider) needed a technical thought partner. Operating at massive global scale, they needed to integrate their data science team to refine critical decision-making tools — move marketing attribution and product experimentation from traditional heuristic models to a high-performance, Bayesian-first framework.

### Project Goals
- **Decode Incremental Growth:** Determine the true "lift" of every marketing dollar spent across TV, Social, and Search.
- **Future-Proof Attribution:** Build a model that didn't rely on invasive tracking, using principled statistical power of Media Mix Modeling (MMM).
- **Accelerate Innovation:** Modernize A/B testing infrastructure to support hundreds of simultaneous experiments without sacrificing mathematical rigor or computational speed.

### Problem
Before collaboration, HelloFresh hit a "technical ceiling" as they scaled. Two major pain points:
- **"The 'Black Box' Struggle":** Standard marketing models struggled with "Adstock" (lingering effect of an ad) and "Saturation" (diminishing returns). In volatile post-pandemic market, these models became increasingly unreliable.
- **"The Computational Wall":** Bayesian A/B testing models were robust but slow. As data volume grew into millions of observations, time required to "run the math" stalled product release cycles.

### Solution
Built a custom library of models tailored to the HelloFresh ecosystem:
- **Principled MMM:** Implemented Bayesian Media Mix Model using custom priors that allowed HelloFresh to bake "expert knowledge" directly into the math. Used Gaussian Processes to allow the model to adapt dynamically to market shocks.
- **Vectorized Inference:** Re-engineered A/B testing logic to a massively parallelized, vectorized approach — allowing the system to process thousands of experimental variations simultaneously. Turned "sequential" process into a "broadcasting" one, drastically optimizing memory and CPU usage.
- **State-of-the-Art Tooling:** Leveraged full power of PyMC ecosystem — ensuring every model was transparent, reproducible, and easily maintained.

### Results
- **60x Faster Insights:** 60-fold increase in speed — transforming hours of computation into mere minutes.
- **Strategic Budget Clarity:** New MMM provided clear "Saturation Curves" — allowing leadership to see exactly where to prune spend and where to double down for maximum ROI.
- **From Internal Tool to Industry Standard:** Work pioneered with HelloFresh became the foundation for PyMC-Marketing, now powering marketing science for companies globally.

## CASE STUDY: Colgate-Palmolive (/work/colgate-palmolive)

**Company:** Colgate-Palmolive
**Services:** Strategy & Technical Advisory
**Year:** 2025
**Website:** https://www.colgatepalmolive.com

### Summary
"We partnered with Colgate-Palmolive to develop a statistically rigorous framework for 'Synthetic Consumers.' By using Large Language Models (LLMs) and our proprietary Semantic Similarity Rating (SSR) method, we enabled the simulation of consumer research at scale, achieving 90% of the reliability of traditional human panels."

### About Project
Colgate-Palmolive partnered with PyMC Labs to pioneer the use of "Synthetic Consumers." Integrated LLMs with Bayesian statistical methods to create digital twins of consumer segments. Resulted in peer-reviewed research and a new methodology for predicting market success at the speed of thought.

### Project Goals
- **Scale Concept Testing:** Evaluate thousands of product ideas instantly.
- **Maintain Statistical Rigor:** Ensure AI-generated feedback mirrors the actual distribution of human opinions.
- **Reduce Innovation Lead Time:** Move from concept to validated idea without weeks-long lag of recruiting human participants.

### Problem
Traditional market research is a bottleneck for modern innovation — high costs and significant time. Standard LLMs were notoriously biased when asked for numerical ratings (Likert scales) — overly agreeable, failing to capture diversity of human sentiment. They provide a single "average" answer, ignoring polarized reality. Without solving this "mode collapse," synthetic data was useless for high-stakes decisions.

### Solution
PyMC Labs developed the **Semantic Similarity Rating (SSR)** method:
- **Bayesian Persona Prompting:** "Prime" LLMs with rich, diverse consumer personas grounded in real-world demographic data.
- **Qualitative Elicitation:** Ask "Synthetic Consumers" to write an honest, narrative review of a product concept.
- **The SSR Engine:** System takes text and uses high-dimensional embeddings to compare it to thousands of "anchor" human responses. Maps sentiment of the text back onto a numerical scale.
- **Uncertainty Quantification:** Using PyMC framework — produces a probability distribution reflecting natural hesitation and variance found in real human populations.

### Results
Benchmarks against **9,000+ human responses across 57 personal care concepts**:
- **90% Reliability:** Synthetic panels reached same level of consistency as human test-retest reliability.
- **Human-Like Distributions:** Successfully broke the "AI bias" — producing response curves accurately reflecting diversity of human opinion, including negative feedback.
- **74% Agreement Rate:** With real-world human panels in identifying "winner" concepts.
- **Strategic Advantage:** Colgate-Palmolive can now "pre-screen" thousands of ideas in minutes, reserving human testing budget for only the most promising AI-validated concepts.
