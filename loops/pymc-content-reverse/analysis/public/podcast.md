### Show Description

> "A podcast on Bayesian statistics hosted by Alexandre Andorra. Deep dives into probabilistic programming, PyMC, Stan, causal inference, and Bayesian modeling with world-leading researchers."

> "When Alex started learning Bayesian methods, he wished there were a podcast to introduce him to the methods, the projects, and the people who made it all possible — so he created Learning Bayesian Statistics."

Guests discuss not only successes but also **failures** — what didn't work and why, because "that's how we learn best."

Topics span: clinical trials, epidemiology, sports analytics, quantum physics, machine learning, causal inference, dark matter, election forecasting, and more.
