## Organizational Philosophy

**Teal Organization model** (from day 1, championed by Thomas Wiecki):
- Inspired by *Reinventing Organizations* by Frederic Laloux — Thomas asked all team members to read it in August 2020
- Reference: Valve Employee Handbook as exemplar of teal org
- Self-organizing teams per client project
- Flat hierarchies — no one tells anyone what to do
- "Individual empowerment to make decisions themselves after conferring with others"
- Thomas: "I also would like us to try and avoid hiring a bunch of managers and rather develop a structure that's more self-organizing (like open source!)" — Jan 2025
- "pymc-labs/pymc-teal" GitHub repo created in Aug 2020 to document organizational principles

**Core principle quote** (Thomas, Aug 2025):
> "At Labs, there are few rigid roles or hierarchies. Nobody's handing you a to-do list. That freedom is the point — but it can also make people hesitate. You might not want to overstep, look like you're posturing, or play power games… so you do less. That's the wrong error mode. This system works when we do more."

## Org Structure (as of 2025–2026)

### Tiers / Membership Status (formalized Mar 2025)

**Explorer Tier** (new/trial):
- 2–6 month onboarding period
- Not included in the bonus pool
- Paired with a buddy for navigation, cultural fit eval, technical assessment
- Can join projects as a contributor
- Thomas Jan 2025: "senior researchers are not part of the bonus but get paid according to our existing system. they get paired with a buddy and the job of the buddy is to help them navigate, find the right project etc but also evaluate for cultural fit, technical skill etc"
- Former name considered: "associate tier" (Chris Fonnesbeck suggestion)

**Core Tier** (full members):
- Included in the bonus pool
- Self-assign issues from GitHub issue tracker
- Work from wherever/whenever — pure output-focused flexibility
- Thomas: all paid as contractors, not employees (legal entity structure)

**Explorer Tier subcategory (2025):**
- "GenAI Incubator" under Nina — has a "Developer" role (Jana confirmed Feb 2025)

### Expertise Levels (formalized Mar 2025)
- **Associate**: Junior/entry-level data scientists
- **Principal**: Senior data scientists — individual page: https://www.notion.so/pymc-labs/Expertise-levels-and-membership-status-1bd838c5de5080669fc8c647338ba493

### Compensation Model
- Hourly billing via Toggl (time tracking)
- Shared bonus pool distributed based on billable hours
- Debates throughout 2025–2026 about incentivizing reliability/high availability
- "Per-hour bonus" is the mechanism — bonus pool grows with company revenue
- $8M revenue in 2025 (from #sales extraction)
- Key tension: flexibility vs. reliability for high-demand projects (Bain, ReadyState)

## Org Values / Culture Notes

**From Thomas (Aug 2020, purpose doc):**
- Build a teal organization (evolved, self-managing)
- Personal growth alongside professional growth
- "Open source heart" — open source contributions are valued and billable
- Win-Win-Win model: good for clients + good for team + good for OSS community

**Flat hierarchy in practice:**
- No mandatory manager approval for work decisions
- GitHub issue tracker self-assignment model (2025)
- "Bazaar-type / open-source model" for projects
- Regular all-team calls on Mondays

**Company values (from pymc-teal repo, 2020):**
- Learning and growth
- Intellectual rigor
- Work-life flexibility
- Open source commitment
- Collaboration without bureaucracy
