## Key Reminders

- **Visa appointment by 2026-04-20** (VFS Manila)
- **Travel insurance ≥€30k** before visa appointment
- **Railjet ticket** book on ÖBB app 2-3 weeks ahead for Sparschiene €21-30
- **Tuxer Guest Card** — hotel gives at check-in, makes Bus 4104 + Sportbus free
- **Snowboard bag** — pack board + boots + helmet + gloves; soft bag preferred for airline acceptance
- **Currency** — Euros in Austria (ATM at Mayrhofen or VIE airport)
- **Power** — Type C/F plugs (Euro standard); bring adapter
- **Language** — German, but tourism areas fluent English
