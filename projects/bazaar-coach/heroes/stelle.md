# Stelle

## Overview

Stelle is a high-flying mechanical genius whose entire kit revolves around the **Flying** mechanic and engineering/tool-based synergies. She has abundant Haste access, lower-than-average cooldowns, and 100+ unique items providing extremely flexible build paths. DLC hero ($20 USD).

**Win condition target**: Build around a Flying-based engine or Destroy loop, scaling into overwhelming late-game power. Most builds pivot at Day 6 when Gold Large items unlock.

## Hero Identity

- **Archetype**: Engineer / Flying mechanic / Destroy loops / Burn
- **Signature traits**: Flying, Tools, Vehicles, Destroy/Repair, Haste, Burn, Shield
- **Item pool size**: 100+ items
- **Flexibility**: Very high — 8+ distinct build paths

## Unique Mechanic: Flying

An item that is **Flying** has Slow and Freeze durations halved. This provides counter-play against disruption-heavy opponents.

- Flying is a status applied to items, can be toggled on/off
- Many Stelle items trigger effects when items **start Flying** or **stop Flying**
- Start-Stop builds exploit rapid Flying state changes for massive scaling
- Flying items are inherently resistant to Slow/Freeze disruption

## Build Archetypes

### 1. LavaRoller (Burn/Destroy) — META TIER
**Win condition**: LavaRoller's passive doubles its own Burn every time it destroys an adjacent item → exponential Burn (thousands per second).

**Core items**: LavaRoller, Parts Picker, Joystick/Ni-Kola, Smoke Detector
**Key support**: Fire Bomb, fast self-Destroy items
**Transition**: Balloon Engine line early → pivot into LavaRoller at Day 6+

**LavaRoller stats**:
- Gold: Burn 40, 10.0s CD, 16g
- Diamond: Burn 80, 8.0s CD, 32g
- Passive: When you Destroy adjacent item, **double this item's Burn for fight**

**Key principle**: The doubling is exponential. Parts Picker creates thousands of Burn/sec. Fiery enchant on LavaRoller doubles Burn further. Shiny on Parts Picker for more Destroys.

### 2. Self-Destroy (Charge > Destroy > Repair Loop) — META TIER
**Win condition**: The Big One + Parts Picker + Repair Drone creates repetitive percentage-based blasts.

**Core items**: The Big One (% Max HP Damage), Parts Picker (destroy engine), Repair Drone (loop enabler), Boosted Saucer
**Key support**: Joystick (charges drones), Hydrogen Tank (on-start Haste)
**Early game**: Boosted Saucer + Fire Bomb + Boom Boom Bot + Recycler Bot

**Key principle**: "Pay close attention to timing. Items must get Repaired BEFORE they would next be Destroyed." Misaligned cooldowns break the combo permanently for the fight. Radiant enchant on key items (immune to Freeze/Slow/Destroy).

### 3. Pillbuggy (Haste/Shield One-Shot) — META TIER
**Win condition**: Pillbuggy gains permanent Shield every time it's Hasted, then deals Damage equal to total Shield. Scales beyond enemy Max Health with Multicast and Crit.

**Pillbuggy stats**:
- Silver: Shield 40, +40 per Haste, 8g
- Gold: Shield 80, +80 per Haste, 16g
- Diamond: Shield 120, +120 per Haste, 32g
- Active: Shield X. Deal Damage equal to your Shield.

**Core items**: Pillbuggy, Anemometer, Radar Dome (Multicast 3), Freefall Simulator, Headset, Marshalling Lights, Propeller/Bird Cage
**Variants**: Radar Dome variant, Launch Tower variant, Stelle's Workshop variant

**Key principle**: Obsidian (double Damage) or Deadly (+50% Crit) on Pillbuggy. Static Acceleration skill is S-tier (Crit per Haste).

### 4. Space Laser (One-Shot Pivot, Day 6+)
**Win condition**: Space Laser deals flat **9999 Damage**. Goal is to accelerate it to fire ASAP through Charge synergies.

**Core items**: Space Laser (9999 Damage), Observatory (self-loops with Flying), Ornithopter (fastest Flying trigger), Altimeter, Propeller, Vortex Cannon
**Early game**: Caracara + Aerial Turret opener → transition at Day 6

**Key principle**: Radiant on Observatory (cannot be disrupted). Obsidian on Space Laser.

### 5. Stellar Swallowtail (Start-Flying/Haste)
**Win condition**: Board-wide Haste to Flying items while self-Charging at peak speeds.

**Core items**: Stellar Swallowtail, Hang Glider, Gatling Gun, Clockwork Disc, Flare Gun, Wrench
**Opener**: Caracara + Aerial Turret → Gyro Gunsight → Headset

### 6. Balloon Engine (Burn/Flying Hybrid)
**Win condition**: Every start-Flying trigger converts to Burn via Balloon Engine.

**Balloon Engine stats**:
- Bronze: Burn 3, 5.0s CD, 4g
- Silver: Burn 5, 5.0s CD, 8g
- Gold: Burn 7, 5.0s CD, 16g
- Diamond: Burn 9, 5.0s CD, 32g

**Core items**: Balloon Engine, Freefall Simulator, Clockwork Disc, Fire Bomb, Headset → Smoke Detector
**Key skills**: Immolating Spark, Final Flame, Conflagration

### 7. Tool/Molten Ball Blaster (Economy)
**Win condition**: Economy-focused tool build converting Tools into raw Damage.

**Core items**: Molten Ball Blaster (obtain from Aila early), Toolbox, Box Cutter, Blow Torch, Wrench/Hammer, Stelle's Workshop
**Key skills**: Workin' Hard, Tools of the Trade, Flashy Mechanic, Grease Fire

### 8. Additional Sub-Archetypes
- **Slow Synergy**: Fire Hose + Flashbang + Steam Hose + Multitool + Lightning Rod
- **Shield Conversion**: Ramming Balloon + Pillbuggy + Windbreaker + Chicken Cannon
- **Drone Builds**: Joystick + Battle Drone + Angle Grinder Drone + Lightning Butterfly + Tantius Carrier
- **Vortex Cannon + Observatory**: Board-wide Flying + Observatory self-charging loop

## Key Items

### Weapons
- **Aerial Turret** (Med, Bronze, 7s CD) — Deal 15/25/35/45 Dmg. While Flying: +1 Multicast. CD reduces 1s per fight use
- **Gatling Gun** (Med, Silver) — Multicast 2. First use halves CD. Gains Crit per use
- **Daggerwing** (Large, Silver, 8s CD) — Gains +100 bonus Dmg when items start/stop Flying
- **Space Laser** (Large, Gold) — Flat 9999 Damage. Self-Charging

### Vehicles & Flying Enablers
- **Ornithopter** (Small, Bronze, 5/4/3/2s CD) — Adjacent item starts Flying. Left Flying item gets -1s CD
- **Freefall Simulator** (Med, Silver) — Swaps adjacent items' Flying status. Essential start-stop piece
- **Anemometer** (Med, Silver, 5/4/3s CD) — Adjacent item starts Flying. Grants 2s Haste on Flying state changes
- **Observatory** (Large, Silver, 8/7/6s CD) — Charge adjacent items 2s. When Flying item used, Charge this 2s. Self-sustaining loop

### Tools
- **Toolbox** (Med, Silver, 7s CD) — Shield 10/30/50/100. Tools CDR 5/10/15/20%. Primary economy item
- **Parts Picker** (Gold, 8/7s CD) — Destroy leftmost item. When destroying, reduce other items' CDs 0.5/1.0s for fight

### Destroy/Repair
- **LavaRoller** (Med, Gold, 10/8s CD) — Destroy adjacent + Burn 40/80. **Doubles Burn on Destroy**
- **The Big One** (Med, Gold) — Percentage Max HP Damage. Self-Destruction synergy

## What to Avoid

- **Do NOT burn all gold before Day 6** — save at least 24g. Stelle's strongest items are Gold-tier Large items. Missing the Day 6 pivot is run-ending.
- **Do NOT refuse to pivot** — Balloon Engine/Chicken Cannon builds often can't carry late. Pivot at Day 6 if not scaling.
- **Do NOT spread resources thin** — 100+ items, 79 skills. Commit to ONE archetype.
- **Do NOT misalign Destroy/Repair timing** — in Self-Destroy builds, items must be Repaired BEFORE next Destroy.
- **Do NOT forget Flying is disruption resistance** — against Slow/Freeze opponents, making key items Flying is survival, not just synergy.
- **Do NOT neglect Toolbox economy** — limited economy options. Toolbox is essential even outside Tool builds.
