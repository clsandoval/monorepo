# Jules

## Overview

Jules is a celebrity chef hero who fights with temperature-based mechanics — Burn via Heated items and Freeze via Chilled items. She has NO Heal items and NO Poison items, relying on Burn and Regen for damage/sustain. She introduced the unique **Sockets** system (Stoves and Coolers) that modify item behavior.

**Win condition target**: Build around a core Food engine, scaling Burn or Freeze damage. Most builds come online by Day 6-7.

## Hero Identity

- **Archetype**: Temperature-based / Food engine / DoT + Freeze control
- **Signature traits**: Burn, Freeze, Food items, Tools, Sockets (Stoves/Coolers), Regen (no Heal/Poison)
- **Unlock**: DLC hero ($20 USD)
- **Flexibility**: High — six distinct build archetypes

## Unique Mechanic: Sockets

Jules has **Stoves** and **Coolers** that modify board items:
- **Stoves**: Benefit items with the **Heated** keyword (reduces cooldowns, amplifies Burn)
- **Coolers**: Benefit items with the **Chilled** keyword (Freeze synergies, damage conversion)

Socket progression:
- **Level 3**: Unlocks Cooler socket
- **Level 9**: Choose Stove OR Cooler socket

Recent patches allow converting Stoves to Coolers and vice versa. Most top builds now run all-Stoves or all-Coolers for maximum synergy.

## Build Archetypes

### 1. Burn Jules (Most Consistent)
**Win condition**: Stack Burn through fast Food cycling with Microwave engine.

**Core items**: Microwave, Rice (early) → Feast (mid) → Grill/Jumbo Wok (late), Scorchpepper, Coffee, Butter, Black Pepper
**Key support**: Pickled Peppers, Hot Sauce, Meat Tenderizer, Universal Sauce, Fortune Cookie
**Late-game capstones**: Grill + Feast, Gingerbread House, Cheese Wheel, Pantry

**Key principle**: Entire Burn core (Microwave + Rice + Scorchpepper) is obtainable Day 1. Scorchpepper goes RIGHT of Rice/Feast. Coffee goes RIGHT of Microwave for self-haste. Heated slot priority on Microwave.

### 2. Freeze Jules (Powerful Pivot, Day 6+)
**Win condition**: Convert Freeze triggers into raw Damage via Walk-In Freezer.

**Core items**: Walk-In Freezer (Chilled: deal 100/200 Dmg when Freezing), Blender, Pastry Cart
**Key support**: Instant Noodles, Butter, Banu Leaves, Sorbet, Ice Cubes
**Late-game capstones**: Diamond Pastry Cart (freezes TWO items — extremely powerful)

**Key principle**: NO clean early-game path. This is a pivot archetype starting Day 6+ when Gold items appear. Don't commit early.

### 3. Fruit Press Jules (One-Shot Potential)
**Win condition**: Exponential damage doubling — Fruit Press destroys leftmost Food to double its own Damage each trigger.

**Core items**: Fruit Press (Silver, 5s CD), Dishwasher, Coffee, Black Pepper, Butter
**Key support**: Instant Noodles, Banu Leaves, Hot Sauce, Pancakes, Baked Potato

**Key principle**: Base Damage on Fruit Press is CRITICAL — doubling is exponential, so starting from 10 Dmg vs 50 Dmg is night and day. Invest in base Damage (skills, Dishwasher) before relying on doubling. Glass cannon — lacks defenses.

### 4. Giant Lollipop Jules (Fast Aggro + Slow Control)
**Win condition**: Fast, high-damage strikes with innate multi-Slow disruption.

**Giant Lollipop stats**:
- Silver: 50 Dmg, Slow 1 for 1s
- Gold: 100 Dmg, Slow 2 for 1s
- Diamond: 150 Dmg, Slow 3 for 1s
- Passive: +Dmg equal to 5% Max HP, +1 Multicast
- Quests: Use 100 Food, Slow 80 times
- CD: 5.0s

**Core items**: Giant Lollipop, Dishwasher, Cutting Board, Excellent Vintage, Pizza Cutter
**Key support**: Bubblegum Floor, Egg Timer, Sorbet, Black Pepper, Weather Machine

**Key principle**: Best enchants are Heavy (2x Slow duration), Deadly (+50% Crit), Shiny (+1 Multicast). Slow and Steady skill is S-tier (scales Damage per Slow).

### 5. Regen / Bread Knife Jules (Scaling One-Shot)
**Win condition**: Stack massive Regen, convert to lethal Damage via Bread Knife.

**Bread Knife**: 4.0s CD. Deal Damage equal to double your Regen. Haste Food to left 1/2/3 sec.

**Core items**: Basket (shield/regen), Bread Knife, Trail Mix, Azakan Stew, Burrito, Jambalaya
**Late-game capstones**: Farmer's Market (massively multiplies Basket/Trail Mix), Veggie Garden, Laurel's Fortress

**Key principle**: Start with Burn scaling early, commit to Regen once Bread Knife appears. Prioritize Ande/Aila vendors for Bread Knife. Economy-intensive.

### 6. Durian / Shield Conversion Jules
**Win condition**: Accumulate massive Shield, convert to burst Damage via Durian.

**Core items**: Durian (unlocked Level 3 with Cooler), Apron, Banu Leaves, Butter, Chef Hat
**Key support**: Laurel's Fortress, Cheese Wheel, Meat Tenderizer, Universal Sauce, Zarlic
**Late-game capstones**: Diamond Cheese Wheel + Diamond Meat Tenderizer (highest peak)

**Key principle**: Consistent from Level 3 onward. Durian is significantly faster than older shield-to-damage converters. Improvised Protection skill is the best scaling enabler. Pivot away if taking early losses.

## Power Spikes

| Day/Level | What happens |
|-----------|-------------|
| Day 1 | Engine assembly — entire Burn core (Microwave + Rice + Scorchpepper) obtainable. Prioritize Mittel → Kina → Lost Crate |
| Level 2 | First skill/enchant. Bronze skills (Final Flame, Immolating Spark, Left-Handed) give huge early advantage |
| Level 3 | **Cooler unlock** — Jules-specific. Enables Durian/Shield Conversion builds immediately |
| Level 5 | Silver skill access — Conflagration, Shatter, Improvised Protection |
| Day 6+ | Gold items available — Freeze pivot window opens (Walk-In Freezer, Grill, Giant Lollipop) |
| Level 8 | Gold skill — Workin' Hard, Big Numbers, Building Crescendo, Grease Fire |
| Level 9 | **Second socket (MAJOR)** — choose Stove or Cooler. "By Level 9 you should be steamrolling" |
| Level 10 | Two random enchantments — transform key items (Obsidian Cheese Wheel, Fiery Grill) |
| Level 13 | **Diamond skill (MASSIVE)** — Moth to a Flame, Living Flame, Knife Tricks, Re-Tooled, Big Guns. ALWAYS reroll if you don't like what you see |

## Item Priority (S-Tier)

- **Dishwasher** — Haste Tools + Weapons gain Damage for fight. Universal enabler.
- **Scorchpepper** — Enables two Heated slots early. Key Microwave pairing.
- **Pastry Cart** — Diamond freezes TWO items. Oppressive.
- **Farmer's Market** — Massively multiplies Basket/Trail Mix scaling.
- **Giant Lollipop** — Fast aggro + multi-Slow. One of Jules's best win conditions.
- **Caviar** — Premium Food item.
- **Excellent Vintage** — Multicast Slow enabler.
- **Pizza Cutter** — Giant Lollipop synergy.
- **Zarlic** — Static Acceleration combo.

## What to Avoid

- **Do NOT commit to Freeze before Day 6** — no clean early path. It's a pivot archetype.
- **Do NOT ignore base Damage on Fruit Press** — doubling from 10 vs 50 is massive.
- **Do NOT skip rerolling at Level 13** — Diamond skills are meta-defining.
- **Do NOT overwrite good enchantments** — new enchantment replaces old, plan carefully.
- **Do NOT ignore Socket positioning** — adapt board to socket placement, not the other way around.
- **Do NOT forget to convert Sockets** — recent patches allow Stove↔Cooler conversion for max synergy.
