# Freediving — equalization & depth

Physical skill, so this file works differently from the others: the roadmap is a training progression,
and `hours_done` should absorb his own drill time, not just tutoring. He reports "did the daily list
4× this week" → log it as `[drill]` and add the hours.

## The hard rule

**Never breath-hold in water without a buddy watching who can pull him out.** Not a pool, not the
shallows, not "just one." Dry drills alone are fine. This is not negotiable and does not get softened.

Also not yet, all downstream of a reliable Frenzel: mouthfill, reverse packing, packing, O2 tables.
Reverse packing on an untrained diaphragm is a shallow squeeze waiting to happen.

## The central diagnosis (2026-07-29)

**Failing at 20ft is not a weak-Frenzel problem.** A working Frenzel runs to 25-30m before air volume
is the binding constraint. At 6m there's enormous air available. So the mechanism is wrong, not weak —
and balloon strength work will not fix a mechanism fault. Four suspects, in order of prior probability:

1. **Head position** — head-down with chin tucked or neck cranked to see the bottom pinches the
   eustachian tubes. Feels identical to "ran out of air."
2. **Too late, too rarely** — waiting to feel pressure. Once the tubes lock under differential, force
   does nothing. Should be ~every metre, continuously, pre-emptively.
3. **Soft palate drift** — palate floats toward the Valsalva position mid-descent and the air can't
   reach the nose regardless of tongue work.
4. **Hybrid technique** — most self-taught "Frenzel" is part chest. Works head-up and shallow, quits
   on inversion.

He has NOT yet been walked through these one at a time. That's the top of the next session.

## Daily drill list (issued 2026-07-29)

~12 min dry, every day. Deliberately conservative — he's 2 months detrained.

1. **Soft palate isolation — 2 min.** Nose-in/mouth-out, switch, alternate fast; then hum→"ng" and hold.
   Learn the three states: palate up (nose sealed), down (mouth sealed), **neutral = the Frenzel
   position**, which is the one most people can't find.
2. **Tongue lock ladder — 2 min.** T (tip at the ridge) and K (back at the palate), 5s holds. T is the
   shallow tool; K is what makes mouthfill possible later.
3. **Balloon drill — 3 min.** Inflate using the TONGUE AS A PISTON only. Hand on the stomach — if it
   moves, that's Valsalva and the rep is wasted. Small pumps, 10-15, rest, ×3. Highest-value item.
4. **Dry Frenzel on command — 3 min.** Nose pinched, mouth shut, tongue only. Target 10 clean pops in
   20s. Then the hard version: **face-down off the edge of a bed, head hanging, chin neutral** — drill
   the position that actually fails.
5. **Diaphragmatic breathing — 2 min.** Belly rises, chest doesn't; exhale ~2× the inhale.

3×/week add: thoracic + diaphragm stretch (~5 min), dry CO2 table (~10 min, seated or lying, never near
water).
