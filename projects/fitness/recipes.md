## Smoked Salmon & Cottage Cheese Protein Pasta
- **Macros (per serving):** ~445 kcal / ~48g protein (pasta ~200/12g + 150g Bulla cottage cheese ~123/18g + 100g smoked salmon ~120/18g; aromatics negligible)
- **Ingredients:** 1 serving protein pasta · 150g Bulla High Protein cottage cheese · 100g smoked salmon · ½ lemon (juice **+ zest**) · 1 tsp Dijon · 1–2 cloves garlic · red pepper flakes + smoked paprika · black pepper · dill/chives · capers (+ splash of brine) · handful spinach · optional 1 tsp olive oil (~+40 cal) · optional everything-bagel seasoning
- **Steps:**
  1. Boil pasta to al dente; reserve a splash of pasta water, drain.
  2. (Optional) Bloom 1 clove grated garlic + a pinch of chili flakes in 1 tsp olive oil — 30s, don't brown. This is the flavor bomb; skip to save 40 cal.
  3. Blend/mash cottage cheese with lemon juice + zest, Dijon, remaining raw garlic, smoked paprika, pepper into a smooth sauce. Loosen with pasta water to coat.
  4. Toss hot pasta through the sauce **off the heat** (don't boil it — keeps it creamy, not grainy). Wilt in spinach + the bloomed garlic oil.
  5. Fold in torn smoked salmon last so it stays silky. Finish with dill/chives, capers + a splash of brine, extra lemon zest, everything-bagel seasoning.
- **Notes:** ~48g protein for ~445 cal — excellent ratio, huge room under the 2000 ceiling. Flavor is nearly calorie-free: lean on zest, Dijon, garlic, chili, capers/brine, herbs. Cottage cheese does the creamy-sauce job with none of the cream calories. Go easy on added salt — smoked salmon + capers already carry it.
