## Targets (the decision rule)

- **Calories ≤ 2000/day** — this is a **CEILING**.
- **Protein ≥ 160 g/day** — this is a **FLOOR** (180 g is optimal but not realistic, don't push it).
- Carbs/fat: flexible, fill whatever's left under the ceiling.

**TDEE estimate:** Mifflin BMR ≈ 2010, light daily training (×1.4) ≈ **~2800 kcal/day**.
At the 2000 ceiling that's a **~800 kcal deficit ≈ ~0.7 kg/week** fat loss. Reasonable, sustainable.

**How to answer any food/macro question:**
1. Protein under 160 g? → add protein. (Shake is the default gap-filler: 24g / 140 kcal per scoop.)
2. Calories under 2000 with room? → adding the shake/food is fine.
3. Calories at/over 2000? → ceiling wins; skip extra unless protein floor is badly missed,
   then pick the leanest protein source.
Floor beats ceiling when both are in tension (hitting protein matters more than a small cal overshoot).

## Training

- **Setup:** home gym — Smith machine, dumbbells, pull-up bar, ab trainer, jump rope,

- **Priority: consistency over intensity.** Travels often, which breaks rigid programs —
  so the program IS "train daily," not any fixed split.
- **Daily session:** 30–40 min = **≥5 min jump rope** + **≥4 lifts**, always full body.
- **Staple lifts:** pull-ups, overhead press, bench, bicep curls, deadlifts, rows.
- **Intensity:** trains at **70–80% deliberately**. 8 yrs judo + overtraining history —
  will not and should not max out. Don't prescribe failure sets / all-out work.

### Rep scheme for hard, low-rep lifts (pull-ups, chin-ups, leg raises)

His low reps are **clean**, not ugly — clean max is ~2–3. Method: **strict-then-assisted**.

- **Strict (strength/skill):** many sets one rep shy of clean max. Add sets, not strain —
  leave 1 in the tank, never grind to true failure every set.
- **Assisted (size):** finish with band- or Smith-assisted sets in the **6–10** range for the
  hypertrophy volume strict triples can't provide.
- **Pull-up template (at clean max 3):** 5–6 × 2 strict (rest ~90s–2min) → 2–3 × 6–8 assisted.
  ~30+ total reps. Do 2–3×/week, not daily (pulling needs recovery).
- **Progression:** 5×2 easy → sets of 3 → 6×3 easy → less assistance / more sets →
  a clean single set of 5 appears → rebuild scheme around 5s. Log strict sets to track the creep.

### Abs

Goal is abs that "pop." Key truth: **no spot reduction** — at ~30% BF no ab training reveals
them; visibility is driven by the **cut** (men see abs ~12–15%). Whether they pop first is mostly
genetics (lower belly goes last) + the deficit.

- **Don't train abs daily/heavy** — they recover like any muscle; daily work is junk volume that
  steals recovery from pulls/press.
- **Don't stop either** — bigger ab muscles look more defined once fat's off, so build them now.
- **Right size: 2–3×/week, progressive overload.** Weighted crunch machine is the better tool
  (add weight = real growth): 2–4 sets, add load over time, leave a rep in tank. Leg raises 2–3
  sets for lower abs.
- **The move:** relentless cut for visibility + 2–3 quality ab sessions/week for size.
