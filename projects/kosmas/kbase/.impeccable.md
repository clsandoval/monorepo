## Design Context

### Users
Internal Kosmas team members — used for looking up consulting materials, client prep info, facility data, and operational docs. The knowledge base should feel premium and on-brand.

### Brand Personality
**Bold, Athletic, Premium**

Kosmas Athletic Ventures Co. is a sports facility consulting company in the Philippines. The brand communicates high-energy competence — think Nike meets boutique consulting.

### Brand Assets
- **Logo**: `../brand/logokosmasfinal.jpg` — angular geometric wordmark with lightning-bolt runner icon
- **Primary Orange**: `#d67e4b` — warm burnt orange from the runner icon
- **Primary Dark**: `#282d27` — near-black with green-warm tint from the wordmark

### Color System
- **Header**: Dark `#282d27` (brand dark)
- **Background**: Warm whites and warm-tinted light grays
- **Text**: Dark charcoal `#282d27`, not pure black
- **Accent**: Brand orange `#d67e4b` used sparingly — active states, focus rings, highlights
- **Neutrals**: Warm/green tint to match the dark brand color
- **Never**: Blue corporate, neon, cyan/purple

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light mode with dark typography and orange accents
- Angular, geometric feel — sharper border-radius (3-5px, not rounded-full)
- Strong visual hierarchy through weight and scale
- Generous whitespace — premium breathing room
- Tool blocks use orange left-border when active

### Typography
- **Headings**: Bebas Neue — bold condensed display, uppercase
- **Body**: Plus Jakarta Sans — clean modern sans
- **Code**: IBM Plex Mono
- Table headers use Bebas Neue uppercase

### Design Principles
1. **Athletic confidence** — Bold, direct, no hesitation
2. **Orange is the spark** — Highlighter, not paintbrush
3. **Angular precision** — Sharp corners, geometric cuts
4. **Breathe** — Premium means space
5. **Internal but impressive** — Good enough to screenshot for a prospect
