## Design Context

### Users
Internal Kosmas team members — used for sales calls, internal reference, and client-facing prep materials. Not end-consumer-facing. Materials need to look sharp enough to screen-share or print for a prospect, but are primarily tools for the team.

### Brand Personality
**Bold, Athletic, Premium**

Kosmas Athletic Ventures Co. is a sports facility consulting company in the Philippines. The brand communicates high-energy competence — think Nike meets boutique consulting. Confidence without arrogance, sporty without being casual.

### Brand Assets
- **Logo**: `brand/logokosmasfinal.jpg` — angular geometric wordmark with lightning-bolt runner icon
- **Primary Orange**: `#d67e4b` / `oklch(62% 0.13 50)` — warm burnt orange from the runner icon
- **Primary Dark**: `#282d27` / `oklch(20% 0.01 145)` — near-black with green-warm tint from the wordmark
- **Logo typography style**: Angular, geometric, sharp-cut sans-serif letterforms

### Color System
- **Background**: Light — clean whites and warm-tinted light grays
- **Text**: Dark charcoal `#282d27`, not pure black
- **Accent**: Brand orange `#d67e4b` used sparingly for CTAs, highlights, key data
- **Neutrals**: Tint toward warm/green to match the dark brand color
- **Never**: Blue corporate palettes, neon accents, cyan/purple gradients

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Light mode** with dark typography and orange accents
- Angular, geometric feel that echoes the logo's sharp letterforms
- Strong visual hierarchy through weight and scale, not decoration
- Generous whitespace — premium breathing room
- Reference vibe: Athletic brand lookbook meets consulting deck

### Anti-References (what Kosmas should NEVER look like)
- Corporate/boring — no sterile PowerPoint layouts or generic blue corporate
- Cheap/amateur — no Canva templates, clip art, busy layouts
- Overly techy — no dark-mode-with-neon, terminal aesthetics, code vibes
- Too playful — no cartoonish elements, keep it serious and premium

### Typography Direction
- Headings: Bold, geometric sans-serif with presence (Bebas Neue or similar condensed display)
- Body: Clean, modern sans-serif with good readability (Plus Jakarta Sans or similar)
- Use scale and weight for hierarchy, not decoration
- Tabular numbers for pricing and data

### Design Principles
1. **Athletic confidence** — Every element should feel like it belongs on a premium sports brand. Strong, direct, no hesitation.
2. **Orange is the spark** — Use the brand orange like a highlighter, not a paintbrush. It marks what matters.
3. **Angular precision** — Echo the logo's geometric cuts in layout choices, dividers, and visual accents. No soft rounded corners everywhere.
4. **Breathe** — Premium means space. Resist the urge to fill every gap. Let content stand on its own.
5. **Internal but impressive** — Even internal tools should look good enough to screenshot and share with a prospect without embarrassment.
